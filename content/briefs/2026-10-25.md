## 🚨 Safety

**Five people were killed and three wounded when gunmen opened fire inside a home on Blode Street in Steenberg at about 20:00 on Saturday 24 October.** Police spokesperson Novela Potelwa said the victims, four men and a woman aged 21 to 33, had been drinking at the house when the unidentified attackers entered and fired several shots; they died at the scene. A man and two women were taken for treatment. Police believe the attack is linked to gang violence, and the Anti-Gang Unit is investigating five counts of murder and three of attempted murder. No arrests had been made by Sunday morning. Western Cape Police Oversight and Community Safety Minister Anroux Marais condemned the shooting and called on police to step up intelligence-led operations against gang leaders and the networks that supply illegal firearms.
{: #steenberg-shooting data-place="Blode Street, Steenberg" }

**A 13-year-old learner has been charged after two 14-year-old pupils were stabbed at Helderberg High School, a private school in Somerset West, at about 07:45 on Friday 23 October.** The Grade 8 learner allegedly confronted four pupils and stabbed two of them; one was wounded in the head and the other in the head and arm, and both were taken to hospital. The learner was charged with two counts of assault with intent to do grievous bodily harm, released, and is expected in the Somerset West Magistrate's Court on Tuesday 27 October, Cape Town ETC reports. The school's deputy principal confirmed what he called a serious incident, and the school asked the public to respect the privacy of the families.
{: #helderberg-stabbing data-place="Helderberg High School, Somerset West" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/helderberg-high-school-stabbing/)

**The City says complaints about illegal fireworks have risen sharply ahead of Diwali and Guy Fawkes, with at least 49 complaints of illegal discharge and 13 of illegal sales in the first three weeks of October, against six and three in the same period of September.** Most complaints come from Manenberg, Eastridge, Beacon Valley, Atlantis, Heideveld, Hanover Park, Belhar and Bonteheuwel. There are no designated fireworks sites in Cape Town; any display needs a SAPS permit and a Fire and Rescue inspection. Fines include R300 for selling fireworks to a child under 16, R200 for setting them off in a building or street, and R1,500 for selling or storing them without a certificate. Illegal sales or use can be reported on 021 480 7700 from a cellphone or through the City app.

## 🏛️ Local government

**Comments on how the City will draw up its next five-year Integrated Development Plan (IDP), its main plan for 2027 to 2032, close today, Sunday 25 October.** The proposed process plan sets out the timetable: community engagement of at least 21 days from November 2026 to January 2027, after the elections; a draft early in 2027 released for at least 21 days of public comment in March and April; and adoption by council in May or June 2027. Comments can be made on the City's online form, by email to NTO5YR@capetown.gov.za, or in writing at a subcouncil office or library. Separately, comments on draft combined rules for ward committees, the elected residents' bodies that advise ward councillors, close on Saturday 31 October.

## 🩺 Health & community

**The City brought support organisations together at Bothasig Community Hall on Friday 23 October to connect people sleeping rough in the northern suburbs with services.** People could get health screenings, counselling, help with identity documents and social grants, information on shelters and housing, and referrals for substance abuse treatment. The event was run by the City's community, arts and culture development department as part of marking World Homeless Day, which fell on 10 October.

## 🌊 Environment

**Two orcas, a large male and a female, have been seen in False Bay after travelling west along the south coast, where they were reportedly linked to attacks on southern right whale calves.** The pair were reported at Kaysers Beach in the Eastern Cape, Plettenberg Bay on 14 October, Knysna, Jongensfontein near Still Bay and Hermanus before reaching False Bay. A land-based whale spotter alerted Simon's Town Boat Company, and during a two-hour encounter marine scientist Dr Simon Elwen took biopsy samples and fitted a satellite tag to the female, Cape Town ETC reports.
{: #orcas-false-bay data-place="False Bay" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/killer-whales-reach-false-bay-after-south-coast-sightings/)

**Two people were arrested at Hout Bay Harbour on Saturday 24 October in an operation against the illegal feeding and harassment of seals.** The Cape of Good Hope SPCA, City Law Enforcement and the national Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment took part. The SPCA said visitors had been encouraged to touch, sit on or pose with seals, and that people, children among them, had been bitten. It asked visitors to watch seals from a safe distance. Charges were not reported.
{: #hout-bay-seals data-place="Hout Bay Harbour" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/hout-bay-harbour-seal-harassment-arrests/)
