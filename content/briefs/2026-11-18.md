## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**The Faure Water Treatment Plant is shut from 07:00 today, Wednesday 18 November, until 18:00 on Thursday 19 November, and the City is asking residents and businesses to use less water while supply is redirected from the Steenbras and Blackheath pipelines, Cape Town ETC reports.** On Thursday, Woodstock, Salt River and Observatory may be without water from 09:00 to 19:00 while a bulk main in Sir Lowry Road is repaired. Store some water beforehand if you live in those areas.

## 🌦️ Weather

**The South African Weather Service has a heat advisory for very hot to extremely hot conditions in the City of Cape Town and on the West Coast today, Wednesday 18 November, The Citizen reports.** SAWS also expects extremely high fire danger in the West Coast and Cape Winelands districts and in Theewaterskloof, Swellendam, Laingsburg and Beaufort West. The advisory continues on Thursday for the Cape Winelands, the Overberg interior and the Karoo, but not for the city. Cape Town ETC forecasts a high of 35°C in the city today with an extreme UV index of 11 and light southerly winds. Drink plenty of water, avoid strenuous activity in the midday sun, and don't light fires outdoors.
{: #heat }

> [The Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/weather/weather-forecast-18-november-2026/), [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/weather/hot-and-sunny-wednesday-weather-forecast/)

## 🚨 Safety

**Police arrested four people aged 16 to 19 at the Baden Powell Drive and N2 intersection on Monday 16 November after officers saw them trying to break the window of a car stopped at the traffic lights, Cape Town ETC reports.** The Khayelitsha sub-district carjacking team, which was running an operation at the intersection after complaints from motorists, recovered an imitation firearm. The four face charges of possessing an imitation firearm and attempted smash-and-grab, and police are checking whether they are linked to earlier incidents there. Police advise motorists to keep valuables out of sight, doors locked and windows closed, and to report suspicious activity to the City's emergency centre on 021 480 7700.
{: #smash-grab data-place="Baden Powell Drive, Khayelitsha" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/four-smash-and-grab-suspects-arrested-in-cape-town/)

**A report by the Global Initiative Against Transnational Organised Crime (GI-TOC), released in October, examines how firearms stolen from police custody reached Cape Flats gangs, People's Post reports.** It centres on the long-running Western Cape High Court case linked to former police colonel Christiaan Prinsloo, who pleaded guilty and whose plea indicated he supplied about 2,400 firearms to the black market; about 2,000 of them allegedly went to Cape Flats gangs. Citing police records, Gun Free SA says 888 of the firearms have been forensically linked to 1,066 murders in the Western Cape, including those of 89 children. The report says the case, more than a decade old and still in court, shows weaknesses in how organised crime is prosecuted. No SAPS response was reported.
{: #firearms }

> [People's Post](https://novanews.co.za/peoplespost/thousands-of-firearms-allegedly-supplied-to-gangs-through-police-networks/)

## 🏠 Housing & development

**Residents of St James are raising concerns about a retirement village proposed for a 1.1-hectare strip of PRASA railway land between the St James tidal pool and Danger Beach, Cape Town ETC reports.** The site, being developed through PRASA's property subsidiary Intersite, includes the Danger Beach car park, the station, the old station master's houses, tennis courts and a former post office, and artist's impressions show buildings of up to four storeys. The Kalk Bay and St James Ratepayers and Residents Association has questions about public access, traffic, parking and the environment, has asked for documents under the Promotion of Access to Information Act, and says PRASA has not yet answered it in substance. The City says no land-use application has been submitted, and that any application would be advertised for public comment.
{: #st-james data-place="St James Tidal Pool, St James" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/residents-rally-against-seaside-retirement-village-plan-in-st-james/)

**Cavendish Square in Claremont is among 18 shopping centres that Vukile Property Fund has agreed to buy from Old Mutual in a portfolio valued at R16.08bn, Cape Town ETC reports.** The Claremont part of the deal also includes Cavendish Connect, Dreyer Street and The Place. The sale still needs approval from competition authorities, regulators and lenders, and could take effect from 1 April 2027 at the earliest. No changes to stores or trading hours have been announced.
{: #cavendish data-place="Cavendish Square, Claremont" }

## 🌊 Environment

**The 44 baboons moved from Simon's Town to the Urban Wildlife Centre at Kogelfontein are settling into their 2.31-hectare enclosure, the joint baboon task team of SANParks, CapeNature and the City says, according to TygerBurger.** All were moved without injury after vets screened each animal, though one elderly female in very poor health was put down. Females were given contraceptive implants. The task team cited persistent conflict with people in Simon's Town, and says more than 530 baboons remain free-ranging on the peninsula. Conservation groups have opposed the move.
{: #baboons data-place="Simon's Town" }
