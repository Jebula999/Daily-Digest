## 🗳️ Elections 2026

**The IEC is due to announce the official results of the 4 November local elections at 18:00 today, Sunday 8 November, Cape Town ETC reports.** With 98% of Cape Town's 829 voting districts counted at 13:06 on Saturday, the IEC dashboard showed the DA on 56.64% of the combined ward and proportional ballots, down from 58.33% in 2021, and the ANC on 13.68%, down from 18.6%. Parties outside the two biggest took about 29.6% of the vote, up from about 23%, led by the Patriotic Alliance (3.79%), the EFF (3.72%) and the Freedom Front Plus (3.43%). Seats have not yet been allocated in the new 235-seat council, up from 231. The PA lost Ward 56, its only Cape Town ward. The IEC's provincial electoral officer, Michael Hendrickse, said all Western Cape results had been captured and audited after a recount in one Breede Valley voting district. Nationally, the IEC had received 197 objections by 15:00 on Saturday; results stay provisional until they are decided.

## 🚨 Safety

**A 31-year-old man was shot dead and two other people were wounded at a property in St Helena Close, Strandfontein, at about 17:30 on Friday 6 November, police said.** The injured were taken for treatment. Police spokesperson FC van Wyk said a murder case and two cases of attempted murder had been opened, and that the circumstances were under investigation. No one had been arrested.
{: #strandfontein data-place="St Helena Close, Strandfontein" }

> [SABC News](https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/one-dead-and-two-injured-in-cape-flats-shooting/)

**The Hawks arrested a 40-year-old Fidelity crewman on Thursday 5 November over a 2025 cash-in-transit robbery in Airport Industria that they believe was staged with the help of two guards, Cape Town ETC reports.** According to Hawks spokesperson Captain Zinzi Hani, the armoured vehicle was boxed in by two vehicles, shots were fired, explosives were placed on it and an undisclosed amount of cash was taken; the robbers fled when a Flying Squad vehicle came across the scene, leaving a VW Polo burnt out. The prosecuting authority decided the driver and the crewman should be charged. The crewman was due in the Bishop Lavis Magistrates' Court on Friday, and the Hawks are still looking for a second person. The allegations have not been tested in court.

## 🩺 Health & community

**SASSA will pay December grants from Wednesday 2 December, with older persons' and war veterans' grants that day, disability grants on Thursday 3 December and children's grants on Friday 4 December, Cape Town ETC reports.** SASSA said people whose grants are under review must respond to official notices within the given time or risk having payments suspended, and suggested checking rather than waiting for an SMS: send "Hi" on WhatsApp to 082 601 0111.
