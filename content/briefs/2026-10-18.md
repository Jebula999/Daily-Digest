## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Parts of Parow will have no water from 10:00 to 22:00 today, Sunday 18 October, while the City repairs a leaking 375mm main in De La Rey Road.** The areas affected are Sanlam Centre, Klipkop, Ravesmead (east and west), Beaconvale, Florida and Parow Valley. The City says water tankers will be available for household use where needed. Separately, Gunners Circle in Epping Industria 1, at the corners of Grenville, Fisher, Hawkins and Jellicoe avenues, is without water from 09:00 to 16:00 today while fire hydrants are replaced.
{: #parow-water data-place="Klipkop, Parow" }

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/planned-water-supply-maintenance-from-17-to-23-october/), [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/planned-water-disruptions-across-cape-town-key-details-for-residents/)

**More planned water work follows this week.** On Wednesday 21 October, parts of Woodstock, including Ravenscraig, Woodlands, Pine, Queens, Salmon and Victoria roads, will have no water from 09:30 to 18:00. From 06:00 on Wednesday 21 October to 14:00 on Friday 23 October, part of the Tygerberg Reservoir pipeline is shut for a meter replacement; the City says Avondale, Plattekloof 1, De Tijger and Loevenstein "may" be affected, though supply is being rerouted. Pressure tests between Tuesday and Thursday could also cause short disruptions in Saxenberg, Tamboerskloof and Khayelitsha. The City advises storing water in clean, sealed containers before planned outages.
{: data-place="Woodstock" }

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/planned-water-supply-maintenance-from-17-to-23-october/)

## 🚨 Safety

**The Hawks have shut down a suspected illegal laboratory making the cough syrup Med-Lemon in Rondebosch, and arrested a 42-year-old man.** The Hawks' narcotics unit and the Flying Squad raided the premises on Friday 16 October and found a machine allegedly used to make the product and boxes of sachets, according to provincial Hawks spokesperson Lt-Col Siyabulela Vukubi. The man is charged under the Medicines and Related Substances Act and is expected in the Wynberg Magistrate's Court on Monday 19 October.
{: #med-lemon-lab data-place="Rondebosch" }

> [People's Post](https://novanews.co.za/peoplespost/rondebosch-clandestine-laboratory-hawks-arrest/), [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/hawks-uncover-suspected-illegal-med-lemon-lab-in-rondebosch/)

**Kuils River detectives are investigating two early-morning home robberies by masked men in the northern suburbs, and no arrests have been made.** According to TygerBurger, four men broke into a home in Protea Village, Brackenfell, at about 03:00 on 6 October, and five men forced open a sliding door in Haasendal at about the same hour on Thursday 15 October, tying up the families inside. Police said the Haasendal suspects carried a crowbar and a screwdriver but no firearms, and that no one was injured. Local security sources suspect the same group, but police have not linked the cases.
{: data-place="Brackenfell" }

## 🗳️ Elections 2026

**The DA is standing by its Ward 108 candidate, Rudi Stevens, after a video that allegedly shows him assaulting his former wife resurfaced.** TygerBurger reports that the woman says the assault case she opened was withdrawn after long delays, and that he recently asked her to make the video "go away". DA metro chairperson JP Smith called the incident, which the party says happened about five years ago, deplorable, but said the party believes in second chances and pointed to Mr Stevens' later community and anti-gender-based-violence work. The woman disputes the party's account. Mr Stevens has not commented directly. Ward 108 includes Sunbird Park.

## 🏠 Housing & development

**The City and the Western Cape Government have both told the Constitutional Court that no affordable housing has been completed in the inner city since 2017.** In July the court ruled in the Tafelberg case that the two governments had failed to deliver social housing in the CBD and Sea Point, and gave them until 2 October to report back. The City says it has built 5,121 affordable units elsewhere and released 14 sites, six in the inner city, that could yield about 7,000 homes, but that none can go ahead without national funding, which it says has fallen by 41% in real terms since 2017. GOOD's Brett Herron said the reports showed no progress in the areas the court named.
{: #inner-city-housing }

> [People's Post](https://novanews.co.za/peoplespost/affordable-housing-concourt-report-cape-town-tafelberg/)

## 🩺 Health & community

**Some patients at public clinics are going home without their chronic medicines, and the provincial health department says stock remains under pressure.** A Strandfontein patient told TygerBurger she received none of her blood-pressure medication and only part of her asthma treatment at the Retreat collection point. Department spokesperson Shimoney Regter blamed manufacturing and shipping delays, regulatory processes and national procurement contracts, and said rebuilding reserves at the Cape Medical Depot will take time. Patients given a medicine that looks different should check with a nurse, pharmacist or doctor before assuming it's the same.
{: #medicine-shortages }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/western-cape-patients-face-chronic-medication-shortages-as-concerns-grow/)

Departments attending include energy, health, safety and security, and the City's jobs and bursary programmes, and young unemployed people can register on the jobseekers' database.
