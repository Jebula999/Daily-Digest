## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Woodstock, Salt River and Observatory may be without water from 09:00 to 19:00 today, Thursday 19 November, while a bulk water main in Sir Lowry Road is repaired, Cape Town ETC reports.** The Faure Water Treatment Plant shutdown also continues until 18:00 today, and the City is asking residents and businesses across the metro to use less water until supply returns to normal. If you live in the affected suburbs, store some water before 09:00.

## 🚨 Safety

**Two men in their thirties were shot dead and three people were wounded, among them a 53-year-old woman, in what police believe was a gang-related attack in Seawinds, Lavender Hill, on Tuesday 17 November, SABC News reports.** Police spokesperson Wesley Twigg said the people in a vehicle approached the victims, opened fire and drove off. No arrests had been reported. Police say they have increased patrols in the area under Operation Prosper. On the evening of Monday 16 November a nine-year-old girl was shot on Military Road in Lavender Hill and is in a stable condition in hospital; Steenberg detectives have opened an attempted murder case, Cape Town ETC reports. Anyone with information can call Crime Stop on 08600 10111 or use the MySAPS app.
{: #seawinds data-place="Seawinds, Cape Town" }

> [SABC News](https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/two-dead-three-wounded-in-suspected-gang-attack-in-seawinds-cape-flats/), [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/nine-year-old-girl-hospitalised-after-shooting-in-lavender-hill/)

**The Wynberg Regional Court on Wednesday 18 November sentenced a 19-year-old man to five years in prison and gave a 17-year-old a wholly suspended three-year sentence for the brick attack on three DA MPs in Philippi East in August 2025, The Citizen reports.** Ian Cameron, Nicholas Gotsell and Lisa-Maré Schickerling were stuck in slow traffic on Eisleben Road after an oversight visit to the Philippi police training academy when the group threw bricks through the car windows; Gotsell suffered a fractured skull and a severed artery. Both accused were convicted of attempted robbery with aggravating circumstances, the older one under a plea agreement. A third suspect died about three months after the attack. The National Prosecuting Authority said it welcomed the sentences.
{: #philippi-mps data-place="Philippi East" }

> [The Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/courts/two-teens-sentenced-for-brick-attack-on-da-mps-in-philippi/)

## 🏛️ Local government

**The City council elected on 4 November holds its first meeting at 10:00 today in the Council Chamber at the Civic Centre.** The City Manager chairs until councillors elect a speaker, who then presides over the election of the executive mayor, executive deputy mayor and chief whip. The DA, which won an outright majority, has nominated Felicity Purchase for speaker and Xanthea Limberg for deputy mayor, People's Post reported. Councillors will also adopt rules of order, a system of delegations, a meeting calendar to December 2027 and a plan for drafting the new term's Integrated Development Plan, the City's main planning document. ## 🏠 Housing & development

**Grassy Park residents have applied to Heritage Western Cape to have the former Gilray Scout campsite on the M5 and Klip Road declared a provincial heritage site, to stop it being sold or developed, GroundUp reports.** The City owns the now-derelict land, which Scouts South Africa leased from the 1950s until it gave notice at the end of 2024. The City says the site's future use is under review and that, if it is found not to be needed, it would be sold through a competitive process. The Save Gilray Committee wants it turned into a community space with recreation, scout camps, gardens and a heritage precinct, linked to Princess Vlei by a green bridge over the M5. Heritage Western Cape says it must consider public representations first and has not set a decision date.

## 🩺 Health & community

**A Cape Town fire platoon commander revived a three-day-old baby boy with CPR at Belhar Fire Station on the evening of Friday 13 November, Cape Town ETC reports.** Cameron Matthee had just finished a shift of more than seven hours at a fire in Symphony Road when the baby was brought in needing urgent help. He started CPR in the station's trauma room, and the baby began crying as his vital signs stabilised; the family then went on to their own doctor.

## 🌊 Environment

**All 20 botanists and restoration ecologists at an October workshop on Lower Tokai Park agreed that Cape Flats Sand Fynbos can be restored on land that was under pines for about 130 years, and backed felling the last plantation there, Dennendal, Daily Maverick reports.** Seventeen of the 20 said the area should go back to fynbos, and 19 called Dennendal a fire risk; one botanist, Professor Eugene Moll, maintains his view that restoration could take 200 years or more. The report from the workshop, convened by former senior environment official Dr Guy Preston, goes to SANParks to inform what happens after the pines are removed. SANParks' response was not reported.
{: #tokai data-place="Lower Tokai Park, Tokai" }

> [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-11-18-finally-the-science-is-settled-on-lower-tokai-fynbos-what-the-experts-say/)
