## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Tamboerskloof, Spanish Farm in Somerset West and Helderrand may have low pressure or no water for part of today, Wednesday 28 October, during zero-pressure tests.** The Tamboerskloof test runs from 10:00 to 17:00 and the other two from 09:00 to 16:00. On Thursday 29 October, the whole of Schaapkraal in Philippi loses water from 09:00 to 18:00 while a faulty meter is replaced. Over the same hours, parts of Woodstock around Victoria Road (Walmer Road to Searle Street) and Albert Road (Aberdeen Road to Barron Road) may have low pressure or no water during work on a main. Gardens (10:00–17:00) and low-lying parts of Khayelitsha (09:00–16:00) have pressure tests that day. On Saturday 31 October, Bofors Circle and nearby streets in Epping Industria 2 are off from 12:00 to 22:00 for a valve replacement. The City advises storing water in clean, sealed containers and posts updates on @CityofCTAlerts.

## 🌊 Environment

**The dams supplying Cape Town were 73.5% full on Tuesday 27 October, against 88.6% at the same time last year, as the winter rainy season comes to an end.** The City's weekly figure on 26 October was 73.7%, down from 74.0% a week earlier. The City generally wants dams at about 80% when the rainy season ends in late October. Earlier this month it asked households to use at least 10% less water through summer and into next winter. At the start of October it said there was no immediate threat to supply.
{: #dam-levels }

> [City of Cape Town: dam levels](https://www.capetown.gov.za/family-and-home/residential-utility-services/residential-water-and-sanitation-services/this-weeks-dam-levels/), [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/cape-town-households-urged-to-cut-water-use-as-dam-levels-remain-below-target/)

**A Woodstock woman was fined R250,000 in the Cape Town Regional Court on 22 October for illegally acquiring and keeping 145 reptiles, including snakes, geckos and lizards.** She pleaded guilty to all six charges. CapeNature said the animals included protected, endangered, alien invasive and prohibited species, found in 2023 at a shop on Lower Main Road in Observatory and at her home. The alternative to the fine is four years in prison. A further two years were suspended for five years, and she may not get a permit to keep wild animals in the Western Cape for five years.
{: #wildlife-fine data-place="Lower Main Road, Observatory" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/cape-town-woman-fined-r250-000-over-illegal-keeping-of-145-reptiles/)

**The developer of the Goats Window safari lodge near Bonnievale was fined R15 million for building in a protected catchment area and clearing endangered renosterveld without environmental approval, GroundUp reported.** VEA Group Holdings pleaded guilty in the Bonnievale Regional Court under a plea deal finalised on 16 October. R10 million of the fine is suspended for five years, and R5 million is to be paid to Stop Rhino Poaching in five instalments. According to GroundUp, the company kept building after a national environment department notice and a Langeberg Municipality order to stop work. It also paid a separate R1.75 million administrative fine to apply for approval after the fact.
{: #lodge-fine data-place="Bonnievale" }

> [GroundUp](https://groundup.org.za/article/luxury-lodge-fined-r15-million-for-flouting-environmental-laws/)

## 🗳️ Elections 2026

**DA mayoral candidate Geordin Hill-Lewis warned on Tuesday 27 October that votes for smaller parties could cost the DA its outright majority in Cape Town and lead to a coalition government.** Launching "Don't risk coalition chaos" posters in Bloubergstrand, he said the party's own polling put it at 51% to 52%. He pointed to coalition-run Tshwane as an example of instability. Daily Maverick reported other polls: an eNCA/MarkData poll put the DA at 52.6% in the metro, with the Patriotic Alliance at 4.9% and the National Coloured Congress at 2.7%. Ipsos had the DA at 46% in September. Polling day is Wednesday 4 November, with special voting on 2 and 3 November.
{: #hill-lewis-coalition data-place="Bloubergstrand" }

> [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-10-27-hill-lewis-warns-small-parties-could-force-a-coalition-government-in-cape-town/), [SABC News](https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/hill-lewis-warns-voters-against-coalition-government-in-cape-town/)

**Six parties set out their housing plans, using Cape Town as the example, in a Daily Maverick panel.** The DA's Eddie Andrews, the deputy mayor, spoke of private investment, less red tape and support for residents building rental units on their own land. The ANC's Cameron Dugmore proposed ending sales of municipal land to foreign buyers and allowing inner-city buildings of up to 20 storeys. ActionSA, Rise Mzansi, the FF+ and the EFF also took part, with proposals ranging from state land audits to serviced plots for self-built homes. Earlier this month GroundUp reported that none of the affordable housing projects approved since 2017 for the CBD, Woodstock, Salt River, Observatory or Sea Point has been completed.
{: #housing-parties }

> [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-10-27-what-each-party-says-about-sas-housing-crisis/), [GroundUp](https://groundup.org.za/article/elections-2026-affordable-housing-has-the-city-of-cape-town-done-all-it-can/)
