## 🚨 Safety

**A seal bit a woman bodyboarding at Strand Beach at about 06:45 on Thursday 12 November and reportedly approached several other people in the water, Cape Town ETC reports.** She was bitten behind the knee and given a preventative rabies injection at hospital; no one else was hurt. The seal later came out onto the sand, where a safety perimeter was set up, and the City's Coastal Management department was on its way to assess the animal, which a local ocean awareness group said did not look healthy. Keep well away from seals, even ones that seem familiar, and don't approach or chase one that looks unwell.

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/aggressive-seal-bites-bodyboarder-at-strand-beach-prompting-safety-warning/)

**Metro Police arrested two men in Goodwood and seized more than 1.5kg of crystal meth, with an estimated street value of R1 million, after a tip-off about a flat, Cape Town ETC and SABC News report.** Officers saw a parcel of drugs thrown from a window, and searches of that flat and a second one linked to a suspect turned up about 300 Mandrax tablets, two firearms (one with its serial number filed off), ammunition and items suspected to be stolen, Metro Police spokesperson Ruth Solomons said. In a separate case in Delft on Monday evening, 9 November, a reckless-driving stop on Hindle Road led to the arrest of a man and a woman with a loaded unlicensed pistol, nearly R55,000 in cash and nearly 1,000 Mandrax tablets, TygerBurger reports.

**The gunman who killed community activist Loyiso Nkohla near Philippi train station in April 2023 has been sentenced to an effective 30 years in prison, the NPA announced on Tuesday 10 November, Daily Maverick reports.** In his plea agreement, Zukisa Tshabile links the killing to a dispute over unpaid security workers at the station, and says a security company owner, who died in police custody in November 2023, paid for the attack; those claims have not been tested in court. Two co-accused appeared in the Wynberg Magistrate's Court on 11 November.

## 🏠 Housing & development

**Five families fighting eviction from historic cottages in Searle Street, District Six, will have to wait until 27 January 2027 for the next step in their appeal, after the matter was postponed in the Western Cape High Court on Thursday 12 November, Cape Town ETC reports.** The cottages were sold to a private developer, who obtained an eviction order in the Cape Town Magistrate's Court in January; the families appealed, and the owner has applied to have the appeal dismissed over delays in getting court transcripts. Their lawyer, Frankl Weber, said the families are open to mediation and have raised expropriation with government; the City is reported as not opposed to mediation, and has said it has no vacant emergency housing in District Six or the CBD, SABC News reports. Residents and supporters picketed outside the court.

## 🏛️ Local government

**DA leader and Cape Town mayor Geordin Hill-Lewis has written to former president Jacob Zuma inviting the MK Party to coalition talks in municipalities where no party won a majority, Cape Town ETC reports.** MK Party negotiator Dali Mpofu confirmed the letter and said the party had accepted, with talks expected within 48 hours. No specific municipalities were named. The DA holds an outright majority in Cape Town, where the new council meets for the first time on Thursday 19 November.

## 🩺 Health & community

**NASA representatives have visited the NSRI in Cape Town to explore how local sea rescue crews could help if astronauts on its Artemis Moon programme had to make an emergency splashdown off the Cape coast, TygerBurger reports.** They discussed recovering crew from an Orion capsule, the hazards of venting fuel, and a possible statement of intent under which NASA would give technical guidance and cover response costs. NSRI chief operating officer Brett Ayres said its offshore craft can reach up to 100 nautical miles out, and any response would be coordinated by the Department of Transport with Navy and Air Force support.
{: #nasa-nsri }

> [TygerBurger](https://novanews.co.za/tygerburger/nasa-visits-nsri-to-explore-possible-astronaut-rescue-off-cape-coast/)

**The Gangstar Café in Mowbray, opened by The Message Trust in 2017, trains former prisoners as baristas and helps them into jobs, GroundUp reports.** Participants do a job-readiness course and barista training, then work at the café or are placed with coffee-shop chains; the organisation said it has had more than 70 graduations this year across courses inside and outside prisons.
{: #gangstar-cafe data-place="Mowbray" }
