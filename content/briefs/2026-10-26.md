## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Parts of Woodstock, Site C in Khayelitsha and Kalkfontein may have low pressure or no water between 09:00 and 16:00 today, Monday 26 October, while the City tests its network.** In Woodstock the test shut-off covers Victoria Road between Walmer Road and Searle Street and Albert Road between Aberdeen Road and Barron Road, and possibly nearby streets; it is repeated on Tuesday, with a full shut-off on Thursday 29 October from 09:00 to 18:00. In Site C and Kalkfontein the City is running zero-pressure tests and says it cannot say exactly which streets will be affected. Leak detection in Racecourse, Kenridge, Kenilworth and Gunnery today should not interrupt supply. The City advises storing water in clean, sealed containers beforehand.

## 🚌 Getting around

**The two right-hand southbound lanes of Jakes Gerwel Drive near Bonteheuwel, between Bofors Circle and the N2, close from about 09:30 today, Monday 26 October, for urgent repairs.** On Friday evening City road crews found that sand had been dug out from beneath Bonteheuwel Bridge, near the railway line, apparently by people living under the bridge, and the digging has damaged the road surface above. Crews worked through the weekend. The southbound lanes stay open through the morning peak, but traffic will be slower near the damaged sections, and the work is expected to be finished by early evening if it stays on schedule. Urban Mobility mayoral committee member Rob Quintas asked motorists to avoid the section if they can, slow down and allow extra time. The City asks residents to report anyone digging under bridges to its call centre.
{: #jakes-gerwel data-place="Bonteheuwel" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/jakes-gerwel-drive-repairs-to-affect-traffic-into-monday-evening/)

**Kloof Road, which links the City Bowl with Camps Bay, Clifton and Sea Point and has been closed since a storm in September 2023, is due to reopen by mid-December, the City said on Sunday.** The section between Kloof Nek Road and Round House Road was closed after its embankment was destabilised, and a further slip followed heavy rain in winter 2024. Most of the work on the two failed embankments is now done: soil nails and gabion walls have been installed, the slopes rebuilt to road level, and stormwater pipes and culverts upgraded. The contractor will now repair the road surface. Quintas said the City is only repairing what was damaged, so no walking or cycling lanes will be added. The road stays closed until the work is finished.
{: #kloof-road data-place="Kloof Road, Camps Bay" }

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/kloof-road-to-open-to-the-public-by-mid-december/)

**The Western Cape Government's R373.7 million upgrade of Sir Lowry's Pass Road (MR108) near Gordon's Bay is turning about 4.6km of single carriageway between the N2 and Faure Marine Drive (R44) into a dual carriageway, with completion expected in April 2028.** Work started in April 2026. The project includes rebuilding 17 intersections, ten of which will get traffic lights, a new roundabout at Faure Marine Drive, extra lanes on Faure Marine Drive, and resurfacing of Kleineryk, Mountain Stream and Aqua roads. Two-way traffic is being kept open with temporary diversions, and the department asks motorists to follow the temporary signs. So far 33 work opportunities have been created against a target of 230, according to the department.
