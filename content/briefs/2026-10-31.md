## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Bofors Circle and possibly nearby streets in Epping Industria 2 will have no water from 12:00 to 22:00 today, Saturday 31 October.** The City is replacing a faulty 375mm valve on the supply main at the corner of Tripper Way and Bofors Circle. It advises storing water in clean, sealed containers.

## 🚨 Safety

**The Western Cape High Court has granted a provisional interdict against the CATA and CODETA taxi organisations, their office bearers and members, barring violence, intimidation, blockades and unlawful route takeovers.** The order, sought by the provincial Mobility Department and effective immediately, also bars damage to property and interference with other transport, and restricts firearms and dangerous weapons at transport facilities and in taxis on affected routes. The organisations must return to court on 20 November at 10:00 to argue why it should not be made final. Separately, the provincial taxi registrar suspended the CATA Langa association for six months after an inquiry into unlawful operations in Epping; its members cannot apply for or renew operating licences during that time. It follows an unofficial go-slow on Friday morning, 30 October, when the department reported no taxis at Nyanga and Wynberg and limited service at Mfuleni, Langa and the Cape Town station deck. The City said most routes were still running. Cape Town ETC reported three dead and six injured in the violence; on Thursday a reported shooting involving a taxi driver closed the R300 near Jakes Gerwel Drive. Cape Town ETC's report gave no reopening date for the Delft interchange, closed on 28 October. Government is due to meet CATA and CODETA leaders on Monday 2 November. Commuters can call the Transport Information Centre on 0800 65 64 63.
{: #taxi-interdict }

The improvement district's 24-hour control room is on 072 792 7168.

## 🏛️ Local government

**Council approved a small adjustments budget and a R182.5 million, five-year water mains contract at its special sitting on Thursday 29 October, and agreed in principle to explore flood insurance that pays out automatically.** It also approved City support for the SA20 cricket final and the Wynberg Family Festival, and a R600,000 grant for car-free Open Streets Sundays ([what council decided](/stories/2026-10-31-special-council-decisions/)). The minutes record nine parties voting against the adjustments budget. Separately, the south-eastern planning tribunal approved rezonings for higher-density housing in Strand and Rustdal and refused one in Tafelsig ([the tribunal's decisions](/stories/2026-10-31-mpt-south-eastern-decisions/)).
{: #council-decided }

> [City of Cape Town Council Hub](https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/councilhubonline/councilandCommitteemeetings)

**About 60 people from 13 organisations picketed outside the Civic Centre on Friday 30 October over housing, safety, planned data centres and the N2 safety wall, GroundUp reported.** The City's head of risk and resilience, Helen Davies, received their memorandum and said she would pass it to the relevant departments. An organiser said members from Delft, Mfuleni and Khayelitsha were afraid to travel to the city because of the taxi violence.

## 🗳️ Elections 2026

**Special voting runs on Monday 2 and Tuesday 3 November from 08:00 to 17:00, for voters whose applications were approved, and polling day is Wednesday 4 November, from 07:00 to 21:00.** Voters need a smart ID card, a green barcoded ID book or a valid temporary identity certificate; driver's licences, passports and photocopies are not accepted. Cape Town voters get two ballots, one for a ward councillor and one for a party on the metro council. Home Affairs is urging people without an ID to apply and collect it before polling day.

## 🩺 Health & community

**A public square in Strand was officially renamed from Ben Friedman Square to Strand Muslim Community Square at a ceremony on Wednesday 28 October.** The local Muslim community, whose roots there go back more than 200 years, asked for the change, which also remembers families removed under apartheid. The square has three mosques. Council approved the name on 30 July after public participation.
