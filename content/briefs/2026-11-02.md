## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Parts of Kuils River and Gordon's Bay face water shut-offs today, Monday 2 November, with work in several more areas on Tuesday 3 November.** Today, Andy Porter Street and nearby streets in Kuils River are off from 09:00 to 16:00, and streets around Dennehof Street in Gordon's Bay may have low pressure or no water from 09:00 to 19:00. Tuesday's shut-offs cover parts of Steenberg and of Fish Hoek around Kommetjie Main Road (08:00–20:00), Wireless Road in Kommetjie (08:00–19:00), the area between De Wet Road, Exeter Lane and Ottery Road in Ottery (09:00–19:30), and Kleinvlei, Spandau, Perm Gardens, Forest Glade, Forest Heights, Park Village, Somerset Heights and Rosedale (09:00–19:00). Pressure tests that may affect supply run in Kalkfontein and Oude Westhof today, and in Fairways, Parkwood, Helderrand, Erinvale and Bellville South on Tuesday. On Thursday 5 November, Brackenfell Heights, Springbok Park and Brackenfell Central are off from 09:00 to 18:00. The City advises storing water in clean, sealed containers and posts updates on @CityofCTAlerts.

## 🚨 Safety

**The minibus taxi industry has confirmed services for this morning's commute after the City and the South African National Taxi Council met on Sunday 1 November.** Mayoral committee member for urban mobility Rob Quintas said the talks gave him confidence that commuters could rely on taxis today. Police and City and provincial safety teams are to stay visible on routes and at ranks and stations through the week. Provincial mobility minister Isaac Sileku is due to visit ranks across the city today; the ranks and times have not been announced. Provincial and City officials were also due to meet CATA and CODETA leaders today. Cape Town ETC puts the toll from the shootings in the route dispute between the two associations at three dead and several wounded. No reopening date for the Delft interchange, closed on 28 October, had been reported by this morning.
{: #taxi-monday }

> [Cape Town ETC: Monday services](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/cape-town-taxi-industry-confirms-services-will-run-for-monday-morning-commute/), [Cape Town ETC: rank visits](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/western-cape-mobility-mec-set-to-visit-cape-town-taxi-ranks/), [Cape Town ETC: talks](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/western-cape-and-city-of-cape-town-ramp-up-efforts-to-tackle-taxi-violence/)

**A 24-year-old man from Observatory went missing while swimming at Silver Sands beach in Betty's Bay on Saturday afternoon, 31 October.** NSRI Kleinmond was called out at about 14:35 after reports of a drowning in progress. Rescue swimmers, an EMS diver, boats and a helicopter did not find him, and police divers continued the search on Sunday. Police have opened an investigation.
{: #bettys-bay data-place="Betty's Bay" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/cape-town-man-missing-after-swimming-incident-at-bettys-bay-beach/)

**NASA has visited the NSRI to discuss how South African rescuers could help if an Orion spacecraft made an emergency splashdown off the Cape, which the NSRI described as an unlikely event.** The NSRI said that in some launch scenarios a capsule could come down off Cape Town about eight minutes after an abort following launch from Florida, possibly as far as 200 nautical miles out. Range and time at sea would be its main limits. A NASA team has also visited Netcare Christiaan Barnard Memorial Hospital as part of planning for the Artemis IV mission in 2028, Cape Town ETC reported.
{: #nasa-nsri }

> [NSRI](https://www.nsri.org.za/2026/10/nasa-checks-in-on-the-nsri-for-astronaut-rescue-planning/), [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/nasa-visits-cape-town-hospital-ahead-of-2028-artemis-iv-moon-mission/)

## 🗳️ Elections 2026

**Special voting runs today and tomorrow, Monday 2 and Tuesday 3 November, from 08:00 to 17:00, for voters whose applications were approved, and polling day is Wednesday 4 November, from 07:00 to 21:00.** Voters approved for a home visit should stay at the address on their application on both days. Officials will not return if they miss someone, but that voter can still vote at their station on Wednesday. Campaigning must end at midnight on Tuesday, and Home Affairs offices will keep the IEC's hours on polling day for people collecting IDs. To check your voting station, SMS your ID number to 32810 or send "Hi" to the IEC on WhatsApp at 060 088 0000.
{: #elections }

> [SAnews: home visits](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/special-voters-urged-remain-home-until-iec-officials-arrive), [SAnews: security](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/natjoints-ready-safeguard-local-government-elections), [IEC: voting stations](https://www.elections.org.za/pw/Elections-And-Results/Voting-Stations-LGE-2026)

**The national security committee NATJOINTS says arrangements are in place for the elections, and named taxi violence in the Western Cape as an operational concern, SAnews reported on Sunday 1 November.** Voting stations have had security assessments, and the army can support police in identified hotspots if needed. The report also cites three election-related murders in the Western Cape, without details. NATJOINTS will hold daily media briefings.
{: #election-security }

> [SAnews](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/natjoints-ready-safeguard-local-government-elections)

## 🌊 Environment

**A case of African horse sickness has been confirmed on one property in the Cederberg, and movement permits for horses leaving the immediate area are suspended until further notice.** Agriculture minister Willie Aucamp said there is no evidence that it has spread beyond the property. Owners planning to move horses are advised to check official updates first.
