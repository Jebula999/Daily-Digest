## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Planned water interruptions continue today, Tuesday 10 November, Cape Town ETC reports.** In Plumstead, Dalziel, Meadow, Montley and Denbigh Roads are without water from 09:00 to 19:30, and Steenberg has a shut-off from 08:00 to 20:00. Parts of Loevenstein, Lotus River, Bantry Bay, Elsies River and Hanover Park are also affected today. Buitengracht Street in the CBD follows on Wednesday 11 November from 09:00 to 18:30, between Military Road and Leeuwen Street. The City advises storing water beforehand and keeping taps closed during the work.

## 🚨 Safety

**Three men were shot dead and a fourth was wounded outside a property in Glen Close, Grassy Park, on Sunday evening, 8 November, SABC News reports.** Police spokesperson FC Van Wyk said the four men, aged 22 to 39, were sitting outside when the occupants of a white Toyota Tazz stopped and opened fire, then drove off. Police suspect the attack was gang-related. The Anti-Gang Unit is investigating, and no arrests had been made by Monday morning.
{: #grassy-park data-place="Glen Close, Grassy Park" }

> [SABC News](https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/three-men-gunned-down-in-grassy-park/)

**A 14-year-old boy died after a taxi hit him while he was riding his bicycle on Mfuli Road in Leiden, Delft, on Friday 6 November, Cape Town ETC reports.** He was taken to hospital in a private vehicle and died there, police said. Delft police have opened a culpable homicide case, and the taxi and its driver were taken to the police station while the crash is investigated.
{: #delft-crash data-place="Leiden, Delft" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/delft-teenager-killed-in-collision-with-taxi-while-riding-bicycle/)

**The City's enforcement agencies arrested 349 people in a week of patrols and operations, the City said on Monday 9 November.** Law Enforcement made 165 arrests, Metro Police 124 and traffic officers 60, 54 of them for drunk driving. Officers recovered six stolen or hijacked vehicles, two firearms, a homemade firearm and 47 rounds of ammunition, and impounded 113 public transport vehicles. In Hanover Park on Sunday morning, eight people were arrested for public violence after a crowd turned on officers making a firearm arrest.
{: #arrests }

## 🏠 Housing & development

**Residents of the Wetland informal settlement in Khayelitsha are living beside sewage-filled sinkholes while repairs to a collapsed bulk sewer pipe drag on, GroundUp reports.** The City said the 700mm pipe has collapsed at several points under the Wetland and SST settlements, where many shacks are built on top of it. A collapse in Wetland has backed up the whole line and paused work in SST, where about 45 metres of pipe is being replaced. Some families have been moved to Green Point in Khayelitsha so that close to 40 shacks can be cleared for construction. The work was due to finish in March 2026; the City now expects it to run into February and March 2027.
{: #sinkholes data-place="Khayelitsha" }

> [GroundUp](https://groundup.org.za/article/i-fear-my-home-will-fall-into-a-sinkhole-says-cape-town-resident/)

## 🩺 Health & community

**An insurer has cancelled a 76-year-old Khayelitsha pensioner's funeral policy and refunded her after GroundUp asked about R205 a month taken from her old-age grant since November 2025.** GroundUp found similar deductions from other older grant recipients. SASSA said recipients do not have to prove they did not agree to a policy: the insurer must show evidence of consent. Anyone who doesn't recognise a deduction can send a free SMS to 34548, visit a SASSA office or contact the insurer.
{: #grant-deductions }

> [GroundUp](https://groundup.org.za/article/insurer-refunds-khayelitsha-pensioner-after-we-query-funeral-policy-deductions/)

**The crypt beneath St George's Cathedral has reopened as the Crypt Memory and Witness Centre for the first time since 2017, Cape Town ETC reports.** Its new exhibition uses photographs and artworks to tell the story of the 13 September 1989 Cape Town Peace March, which an estimated 30,000 people joined in defiance of a government ban, led by Archbishop Desmond Tutu with other faith leaders and then-mayor Gordon Oliver.
{: #crypt data-place="St George's Cathedral, Cape Town" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/st-georges-cathedral-crypt-reopens-with-1989-peace-march-exhibition/)
