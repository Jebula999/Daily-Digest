## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Parts of Woodstock will have no water from 09:30 to 18:00 today, Wednesday 21 October, while the City replaces pipes and valves.** Affected streets include Ravenscraig, Woodlands, Pine, Queens, Salmon and Victoria roads and Warwick Street, and possibly streets nearby. Separately, pressure testing between 10:00 and 17:00 today and tomorrow may leave some homes in Tamboerskloof and Gardens with low pressure or no water. The City advises storing water in clean, sealed containers beforehand and keeping taps closed until supply returns; water may look cloudy for a while afterwards.
{: #woodstock-water data-place="Woodstock" }

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/planned-water-supply-maintenance-from-17-to-23-october/)

## 🚨 Safety

**A fire broke out at a guesthouse on Thornhill Road, Green Point, at about 20:55 on Monday 19 October, and neighbours used garden hoses to fight it until firefighters arrived.** Cape Town ETC reports that residents of a neighbouring building climbed onto roofs to help, and that the fire was brought under control. Reports that no one was hurt have not been confirmed, and no cause has been given.
{: data-place="Thornhill Road, Green Point" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/green-point-guesthouse-fire-sees-neighbours-step-in-to-help/)

**The inquest into the death of Anele Tembe, who fell from the 10th floor of the Pepperclub Hotel in Cape Town in April 2021, heard toxicology evidence on Tuesday.** A pathologist testified that tests found a blood-alcohol level just above the legal limit, cocaine and traces of psychiatric medication, but that in her view Tembe was not severely impaired and could have been physically able to climb over the balcony. She said neck injuries could not be conclusively linked to strangulation or assault. The inquest resumes on 17 November.

> [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-10-21-anele-tembe-could-climb-over-balcony-despite-cocaine-and-alcohol-toxicology-evidence-inquiry-hears/), [The Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/anele-tembe-inquest-cocaine-alcohol-aka-father/)

## 🏛️ Local government

**The City says its Business Hub productivity programme has helped 68 businesses keep 2,139 jobs since July 2023.** The Productivity Efficiency Programme works with firms on how they run, and the Business Hub also advises on doing business with the City, procurement, going digital and entering new markets. Economic Growth Mayco member James Vos said support should be judged by whether entrepreneurs come away better able to run their businesses. The hub can be reached on 021 417 4043.

## 🏠 Housing & development

**More than 13 years after court-supervised mediation began, families at the Siyahlala informal settlement in Dunoon, built partly on a Transnet railway line, are still waiting to be relocated.** A fire on 4 October destroyed about 20 homes and displaced about 100 people, the second in three months according to the ward councillor, GroundUp reports. Freight trains stopped using the line in 2018. The provincial government has agreed to allow 500 houses for Siyahlala residents at the Killarney Gardens development, but that agreement still has to be finalised through the courts. The City said mediation continues under a supervising judge and declined further comment because the matter is before the court; Transnet said its eviction case is on hold. No party gave a timeline.
{: #siyahlala data-place="Dunoon" }

> [GroundUp](https://groundup.org.za/article/siyahlala-fires-and-overcrowding-as-relocation-talks-drag-into-a-second-decade/)

## 🚌 Getting around

**The temporary traffic circle at the Govan Mbeki Road (M9), Jan Smuts Drive (M17) and Strandfontein Road intersection in Lansdowne will be closed to all traffic from 13:00 on Friday 23 October until 04:00 on Monday 26 October.** The contractor will connect the bridge ramps to the elevated Sky Circle and install new traffic signals, which will replace the temporary circle from Monday. The City has not named detour routes; it asks road users to avoid the intersection and plan for disruption, and says signs and flag staff will be on site. The raised circle will carry MyCiTi buses once that route starts. The Lansdowne project began in July 2022 and is due to finish in April 2027.
{: #sky-circle data-place="Lansdowne" }

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/city-to-close-sky-circle-intersection-this-coming-weekend/)

**Work to ease congestion on the outbound M3 in Newlands, between Upper Sidmouth Avenue and Struben Road, began on Tuesday 20 October.** The first phase widens the road shoulder and footway and upgrades the Newlands Avenue and Rhodes Avenue intersection, and is due to finish on 18 January 2027, with a break over the holidays. Work runs from 07:00 to 17:00, Monday to Saturday. From 16 November to 21 January there will be a partial lane closure on the approach to the Newlands Avenue intersection, with work there limited to 10:00 to 15:00 on working days.
{: #m3-newlands data-place="Newlands" }

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/city-to-commence-with-the-upgrading-of-m3-in-newlands/)

## 🩺 Health & community

**BM Section informal settlement in Khayelitsha has 100 upgraded flush toilets and 20 taps, connected to the City's sewer network, after a R1.7 million investment.** Water and Sanitation Mayco member Zahid Badroodien handed them over and asked residents to use them correctly. Janitors employed through the Expanded Public Works Programme will clean the facilities and report faults. The City says it has provided 368 toilets across communities since July.
{: data-place="Khayelitsha" }

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/bm-section-gets-100-upgraded-flush-toilets/)

**A court case over a Khoisan family's traditional hut at the Castle of Good Hope has been postponed to 10 November.** The family obtained a spoliation order after the hut was demolished, directing the Castle to restore their possession, return their belongings and let them rebuild; the order does not decide whether they may stay permanently. The Castle's management is challenging it, and some Khoisan traditional leaders have called for the hut to be removed from the national heritage site, Cape Town ETC reports.
{: data-place="Castle of Good Hope" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/castle-of-good-hope-dispute-over-khoisan-familys-hut-heads-to-court/)
