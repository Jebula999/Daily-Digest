## 🚨 Safety

**Police arrested three men and seized four firearms in separate operations in Scottsdene, Kraaifontein and Blue Downs, SAPS said on Friday.** In Scottsdene, a 25-year-old was arrested in connection with an attempted murder at Ashbury Court on Sunday 11 October, and a firearm was found abandoned nearby. In Peerless Park, Kraaifontein, a 48-year-old was arrested with two unlicensed firearms and ammunition after a tip-off about suspected gang activity. In Fountain Head, Blue Downs, a 24-year-old was arrested for possessing a prohibited firearm and ammunition. The men are expected in the Bellville and Blue Downs magistrates' courts once charged.

## 🏠 Housing & development

**The City's night shelter in Kensington can now sleep 200 people, up from 80, after a R12 million upgrade.** Mayor Geordin Hill-Lewis opened the expanded facility on Thursday 15 October. Besides the extra beds, the work covered the kitchen, bathrooms, floors and fire safety. The City owns and maintains the building, and Haven Night Shelter runs it. The opening follows the City's new targets to reduce rough sleeping by a quarter by 2030, reported in [yesterday's brief](/briefs/2026-10-16/#homelessness-targets).

## 🚌 Getting around

**The City has started resurfacing roads in Paarden Eiland, Epping Industrial, Maitland, Kensington, Thornton, Windermere and Langa, and the work will run until the end of the year, weather permitting.** Crews work Monday to Saturday from 07:00 to 17:00. Some narrower roads will be fully closed during those hours and reopen after 17:00; on others, half the road will stay open. Roads on the list include Paarden Eiland Road, Marine Drive and Section Road in Paarden Eiland, Alexandra, Coronation and Royal roads in Maitland, Avro and Hampden avenues in Kensington, and Gunners Circle and Fisher Avenue in Epping. Mayoral committee member Rob Quintas asked drivers to allow extra time and follow the signs.

## 🌊 Environment

**Eight Cape Town beaches have kept full Blue Flag status for the 2026/27 season, and four more are being assessed as pilot sites.** The full-status beaches are Bikini Beach, Camps Bay, Clifton 4th, Fish Hoek, Llandudno, Melkbosstrand, Muizenberg and Silwerstroomstrand. Chapman's Peak (Hout Bay), Big Bay, Strand and Strandfontein are pilot sites, which will be checked against the international criteria for water quality, safety, amenities and environmental education. The awards were made in Hermanus on 8 October. The City also says Blaauwberg Nature Reserve has received Green Coast status for the seventh time.
