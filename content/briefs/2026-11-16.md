## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Western Cape Local Government and Environmental Affairs Minister Anton Bredell has advised municipalities to introduce Level 1 water restrictions by 15 November, and to move higher-risk supply systems to Level 2 by 1 December, Cape Town ETC reports.** Cape Town's six main dams held 71.3% on 12 November; the weekly reading of 71.7% on 9 November compares with 85.4% in the same week of 2025. Klipheuwel Dam is at 7.6% and Steenbras Lower at 44.8%. The City says its water status is "Early Drought Caution", not a supply emergency, and that no formal restrictions are in force; it wants daily use below 860 million litres. The permanent water by-law rules still apply: water gardens only before 09:00 or after 18:00, use a trigger nozzle on hosepipes, cover pools when not in use and don't hose down paving with drinking water. Watch the City's official notices for any change.
{: #water-restrictions }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/breaking-urgent-action-needed-to-avoid-full-blown-drought/)

**The City's planned water work this week includes a shutdown of the Faure Water Treatment Plant from 07:00 on Wednesday 18 November to 18:00 on Thursday 19 November, when residents and businesses are asked to use less water, Cape Town ETC reports.** Today, Monday 16 November, pressure testing from 09:00 to 16:00 may cause low pressure or brief cuts in Welcome Glen and Da Gama Park. On Tuesday 17 November, parts of Lotus River and Grassy Park are due to be without water from 09:00 to 18:00, and Welgedacht, Selborne Slot, Oude Westhof, Van Riebeeckshof and Kanonberg from 20:00 to 05:00 on Wednesday. Woodstock, Salt River and Observatory may be without water on Thursday 19 November from 09:00 to 19:00 while a bulk main in Sir Lowry Road is repaired. Store some water before work in your area.
{: #water-work }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/cape-town-water-disruptions-affected-areas-from-14-20-november/)

## 🌦️ Weather

**The South African Weather Service has issued a warning for damaging coastal winds over the south-west coast and the City of Cape Town District today, Monday 16 November, The Citizen reports.** The report does not give a warning level or hours. Cape Town ETC's forecast expects an increasingly windy, sunny day with gusts of up to 76km/h. Secure loose items outdoors, take care on exposed roads, and check conditions before going out on the water.
{: #wind-warning }

> [The Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/weather/weather-forecast-16-november-2026/), [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/weather/cape-town-braces-for-gusty-winds-and-bright-sun-monday-weather-forecast/)

## 🚨 Safety

**Police from Operation Prosper found drugs with an estimated value of R970,850 in a bag hidden inside a couch at a house in Joyce Street, Goodwood Estate, at about 04:30 on Saturday 14 November, Cape Town ETC reports.** According to SAPS, the haul included 1.6kg of cocaine, 27g of crystal meth and 14 ecstasy tablets, and cash and two cellphones were also seized. A 57-year-old man and a 30-year-old woman were arrested on drug dealing charges and are due in the Goodwood Magistrate's Court today, Monday 16 November.
