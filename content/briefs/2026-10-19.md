## 🚨 Safety

**Two men charged with murder over Friday's taxi-related shooting in Epping are expected in the Cape Town Magistrate's Court today, Monday 19 October.** Two men, aged 45 and 46, were shot dead in Fisher Avenue on Friday 16 October. According to the police account reported by Cape Town ETC, a Toyota Quantum tried to block the victims' car and several men got out and opened fire. The Provincial Taxi Violence Unit arrested two suspects, aged 27 and 31, who police say have links to the taxi industry. Police expect more arrests. Cape Town ETC describes the victims as taxi owners, while the police statement calls them taxi association patrol members.
{: #epping-shooting }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/murder-charges-epping-taxi-shooting/)

**Alleged 28s gang leader Ralph Stanfield, his wife Nicole Johnson and 12 co-accused are due in the Western Cape High Court this week for an organised-crime trial.** According to amaBhungane, writing in Daily Maverick, they face 41 counts under the Prevention of Organised Crime Act, including murder, attempted murder, fraud, money laundering, robbery and illegal possession of firearms. The State alleges the couple used violence, extortion and corruption to win construction and infrastructure contracts. The trial is expected to last months, with dozens of witnesses. The accused have not yet pleaded, and the allegations have not been tested in court.
{: #stanfield-trial }

> [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-10-19-analysis-cape-town-gang-boss-ralph-stanfields-courtroom-showdown-looms/)

**The Simon's Town naval base has asked the public to stay clear of the coast between Boulders Beach and Elsie's Peak from 09:00 to 13:00 today because of military activity.** The advisory applies to boat owners, water users and beachgoers. The base's media liaison office can be reached on 021 787 4735.

> [Cape Town ETC](https://capetownetc.com/news/naval-activity-advisory-coastline)

## 🏛️ Local government

**Belhar Main Road in Highbury, Kuils River, has been renamed John Van de Rheede Drive.** Van de Rheede was a teacher at Bridgetown and Belhar high schools, an anti-apartheid activist and a community leader who helped start Highbury's first neighbourhood watch. He died three years ago. The Highbury Foundation asked for the change, and 857 of the 960 public comments received in 2025 supported it. The City says road signs will be updated soon.

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/street-renaming-in-highbury-kuils-river-celebrating-john-van-de-rheede-s-legacy/)

## 🗳️ Elections 2026

**ANC president Cyril Ramaphosa campaigned in Khayelitsha on Sunday, saying the ANC is aiming to win Cape Town outright on 4 November.** Speaking at a church in Khayelitsha, he said townships receive worse services than wealthier suburbs, that apartheid-era spatial planning persists, and that housing money had been returned to national government unspent, according to The Citizen. The Citizen's report did not include a response from the DA or the City.
{: #ramaphosa-khayelitsha }

> [The Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/elections/apartheid-still-exists-western-cape-ramaphosa-anc-eyes-election-victory/)

## 🏠 Housing & development

**Rebuilding Parliament's fire-damaged National Assembly and Old Assembly buildings is behind schedule, with completion now expected in June 2027.** An official from the Auditor-General's office told SABC News the delays came from unforeseen site conditions and related contractor claims, and that the office had found weak financial controls on the project.

> [SABC News](https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/reconstruction-of-parliaments-fire-damaged-buildings-delayed/)

## 🩺 Health & community

**The Western Cape accounts for 18 of the 26 mpox cases confirmed in South Africa since January, the national Department of Health said on Sunday.** Four new cases were added nationally. Of the provincial cases, 16 were linked to local transmission and two were visitors infected abroad. The department advises anyone with a rash, fever, headache, muscle aches or swollen lymph nodes to seek care promptly, and close contacts of a case to watch for symptoms for 21 days and avoid sharing bedding, towels or clothing.
{: #mpox }

> [SAnews](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/four-new-mpox-cases-identified-south-africa), [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/south-africa-confirms-four-new-mpox-cases-as-western-cape-remains-hardest-hit/)

**The City is asking residents to give to registered shelters and organisations rather than directly to people living on the street.** The "Give Responsibly" call accompanies a 21-day running challenge by a former rough sleeper. The City says donations to shelters pay for meals, beds and showers as well as counselling, rehabilitation and skills training. More information is at capetown.gov.za/homeless.

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/city-calls-on-residents-to-give-responsibly-as-former-rough-sleeper-takes-on-21km-challenge/)
