## 🚌 Getting around

**No stopping will be allowed on Parade, Adderley, Parliament, Corporation, Buitenkant, Church, Longmarket and Barrack streets from 16:00 on Saturday 28 November until midnight on Monday 30 November, for the Festive Lights Switch-On on the Grand Parade, Cape Town ETC reports.** Darling, Adderley, Longmarket and surrounding streets close from 05:00 on Sunday 29 November until 05:00 on Monday, and a lane of Darling Street between Parade and Corporation streets stays closed until Tuesday 1 December for the stage. On Sunday the last MyCiTi D01 and D02 buses from the Civic Centre leave at 23:00, as does the last D04 to Kapteinsklip, and the T01 to Dunoon runs until 22:30; route 106 is diverted via Strand Street and will not stop at Groote Kerk. E-hailing pick-up points are on Darling Street outside the Castle of Good Hope and at Strand and Adderley streets. Alcohol, umbrellas, gazebos, fireworks and weapons are not allowed in.

## 🚨 Safety

**More than 100 women, children and men marched from the Khanyisa Centre to the Browns Farm hall in Philippi on Wednesday 25 November, calling for an end to crime and gender-based violence, GroundUp reports.** The march, organised by the SAFE Philippi Platform with support from the MOSAIC Training, Service and Healing Centre, was part of the 16 Days of Activism. Residents spoke about shootings, extortion and domestic violence, and at the hall discussed support for survivors, protection orders and access to justice. A MOSAIC representative said protection orders only work if they are enforced. In the SAPS crime figures for July to September, released on 20 November, Philippi East recorded 41 murders, 13th highest of any station in the country, and the most cases of illegal firearm and ammunition possession, according to GroundUp.

## 🏛️ Local government

**The City's south-eastern Municipal Planning Tribunal on Tuesday 24 November refused an application to rezone 2.3 hectares of farmland on the corner of Sir Lowry's Pass Road and Hydro Road, near Gordon's Bay, for business use.** The application would have needed a departure from the City's spatial plans for the Helderberg. The tribunal approved 30 senior living units in Golden Acre, Somerset West, a business rezoning on Andries Pretorius Street, a farm rezoning in Faure and a substation site in Wildwood. It also set penalties for unauthorised building and land use, the largest R60,000 for flats in Rosedale, Klein Vlei, decided by three votes to one. ## 🌊 Environment

**The faith-based environmental organisation Safcei says it resigned from the City's Baboon Advisory Group on 26 November in protest at the permanent captivity of baboons moved from Simon's Town, according to an opinion piece by the organisation in Daily Maverick.** Safcei says the group did not properly consider expert advice on governance, transparency and animal welfare, and that the joint task team of the City, SANParks and CapeNature treated removal as a foregone conclusion. It is calling for a halt to further captures, publication of the permits and welfare assessments for the Kogelfontein site, and an independent review of non-lethal alternatives. The task team said last week that 44 baboons from the Waterfall and Seaforth troops had been moved to the Urban Wildlife Centre at Kogelfontein, citing persistent conflict with residents.
