## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Parts of Woodstock, Schaapkraal, Beacon Valley, Bellville South, Mfuleni and Kommetjie will have no water or low pressure for part of today, Tuesday 27 October, during planned City maintenance.** In Woodstock a test shut-off from 09:00 to 16:00 covers Victoria Road between Walmer Road and Searle Street, Albert Road between Aberdeen Road and Barron Road and nearby streets. Schaapkraal in Philippi is off from 11:00 to 13:00. In Beacon Valley, Trampoline Street is off from 09:00 to 18:00; Sacks Circle in Bellville South from 09:00 to 17:00; Lukhanyo, Main, Tembalethu and Uluntu streets in Mfuleni from 08:00 to 17:00; and Wireless Road in Kommetjie from 08:00 to 19:00. The City will send water tankers to Beacon Valley and Mfuleni. Bloekombos and Wallacedene lose water from 18:00 until reservoir repairs are done, and Saxenberg Park may have low pressure from 09:00 to 16:00. On Wednesday 28 October, Tamboerskloof, Spanish Farm in Somerset West and Helderrand have pressure tests. The City advises storing water in clean, sealed containers.

## 🚨 Safety

**Detectives from a police unit that investigates taxi violence arrested several suspects over the weekend in the Overberg, Kensington and Langa, in connection with a recent run of fatal attacks in Hermanus and Cape Town.** Police spokesperson Novela Potelwa said the suspects face charges including murder, attempted murder and arson, and were due in several courts on Monday 26 October. Police did not say how many people were arrested. Last week the Western Cape Government said it would investigate taxi associations linked to shootings in Somerset West, Khayelitsha, Epping, Delft, Bonteheuwel and Maitland.
{: #taxi-arrests }

> [SABC News](https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/suspects-arrested-over-western-cape-taxi-violence/)

**Rescuers responded to seven separate incidents on Table Mountain on Saturday 24 October, the last of which ran until about 04:30 on Sunday.** The most serious involved a 24-year-old man who fell about four metres while scrambling on cliffs above Union Ravine and was stranded on a ledge; rescuers abseiled down and raised him about 130 metres by rope, then sheltered him near the upper cable station until he could be brought down. A 56-year-old visitor who collapsed in Platteklip Gorge and an injured young hiker were airlifted, and teams also helped a man with severe cramps, a lost hiker and a woman with a twisted ankle in Cecilia Forest. Provincial EMS, Air Mercy Services, Table Mountain National Park rangers and the Cableway were involved. Rescuers ask hikers to prepare properly, tell someone their route and call for help early.
{: #table-mountain data-place="Table Mountain" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/rescue-teams-face-an-unprecedented-seven-calls-on-table-mountain-in-one-day/)

**About 100 people marched through Makhaza in Khayelitsha to the police station on Saturday 24 October, calling for urgent action against gun violence.** Community policing forum members and neighbourhood watches joined the "Silencing the Gun" walk. Police figures quoted by GroundUp show 1,547 crimes recorded at Khayelitsha police station between April and June 2026, up from 1,522 a year earlier. In September, three men were shot dead in a car in Town Two and four men were killed in Site C; police said the motives were unknown and no arrests had been made.
{: #khayelitsha-march data-place="Makhaza, Khayelitsha" }

> [GroundUp](https://groundup.org.za/article/youd-swear-everyone-owns-a-gun-says-khayelitsha-police-forum-member/)

## 🏛️ Local government

**A special council meeting on Thursday 29 October will vote on a small adjustments budget, a plan to explore flood insurance that pays out automatically, and up to R2.5 million a season in City support for the SA20 cricket final.** The adjustments budget adds R6 million in national disaster funding to repair community facilities damaged in the April and May floods. Council is also asked to approve a R182.5 million, five-year contract for new water mains and hydrants, a three-year deal for the Wynberg Family Festival and a R600,000 grant for car-free Open Streets Sundays on Bree Street and in Langa until March ([what's on the agenda](/stories/2026-10-27-special-council-29-october-preview/)).
