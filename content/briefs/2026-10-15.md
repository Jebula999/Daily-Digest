## 🗳️ Elections 2026

**The IEC has approved a record 2,655,067 special vote applications for the 4 November local government elections.** That's up 131% on 2021. Of these, 678,525 are home visits for voters who can't travel because of illness or disability; the rest will vote at their voting stations a day or two early. Special voting takes place on **Monday 2 and Tuesday 3 November**. The Western Cape accounts for 291,866 approved applications so far.
{: #special-votes }

## 🚨 Safety

**A man died when fire swept through the Singabalapha informal settlement in Observatory early on Wednesday.** City Fire and Rescue said at least three structures were destroyed and the fire was out by 03:55. Several residents were displaced. The cause isn't yet known, and formal identification of the man awaits DNA results.
{: #obs-fire data-place="Observatory" }

> [GroundUp, via Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/man-dies-in-fire-in-observatory-informal-settlement/)

**Five kayakers lost in thick fog were rescued off Clifton on Tuesday morning.** The group, two local women and three German tourists, launched from Three Anchor Bay and drifted about 1.3 nautical miles offshore near Saunders Rocks. One of them raised the alarm through the NSRI's free SafeTRX app. All five were treated for mild hypothermia, and the NSRI is urging every paddler in a group to use the app.
{: data-place="Clifton" }

> [The Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/five-kayakers-german-tourists-rescued-dense-fog-ordeal-cape-town-coast/)

## 🏛️ Local government

**The north-eastern planning tribunal approved blocks of flats in Belgravia, Bellville, and set nine penalties for unauthorised building, from R1,400 to R150,000.** The tribunal met on Tuesday. It also approved offices in Panorama, a guest house in Vredelust and a business rezoning in Monte Vista. [Read what they decided](/stories/2026-10-15-mpt-north-eastern-decisions/).

## 🏠 Housing & development

**The City has told the Western Cape High Court it plans about 28,400 affordable homes, but none of them are finished, a GroundUp analysis found.** Only one inner-city site, Salt River Market, has broken ground, and construction there is due to start in January 2027. Of 13 City-owned parcels released near the CBD in 2017, none has been developed. Mayor Geordin Hill-Lewis told GroundUp that getting projects through the state is very difficult and has ruled out rent control. The analysis also looks at the 72-hectare Annandale site on the N7, where a mixed-income development is proposed. It says residents of neighbouring Dunoon are unlikely to benefit.
{: #housing-pipeline }

> [GroundUp](https://groundup.org.za/article/elections-2026-affordable-housing-has-the-city-of-cape-town-done-all-it-can/)

## 🩺 Health & community

**Social grant dates have been brought forward around the election.** SASSA says older persons' grants will be paid on Friday 30 October, disability grants on Monday 2 November and children's grants on Tuesday 3 November.

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/sassa-adjusts-november-grant-payment-dates-what-beneficiaries-need-to-know/)

**Adults who rely on free incontinence products from clinics say they are getting far too few.** Disability Revolution and #UniteBehind want a clear, public policy from the Western Cape health department and the City. The City says no one with an assessed need is turned away, and that patients can ask to be reassessed.

> [GroundUp](https://groundup.org.za/article/disabled-people-say-clinics-give-them-too-few-nappies/)

**South African children aged 15 and under can visit Robben Island free until 31 October** when accompanied by a paying adult, on the 09:00, 11:00 and 13:00 tours. Check the terms when booking on the Robben Island Museum website.
{: data-place="Robben Island" }
