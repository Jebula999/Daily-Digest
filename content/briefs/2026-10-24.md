## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Parts of Clifton and Stikland have no water today, Saturday 24 October, and the City has scheduled more shut-offs across the city next week.** In Clifton, Nettleton, Victoria, Kloof and Lower Kloof streets and Biskop Steps are off from 09:30 to 17:30 for a leak repair; in Stikland, Labelle Road, Traka, Kariga, Hydro and Ampere streets and part of Cecil Morgan Street are off from 09:00 to 18:00 for new pipe connections. On Tuesday 27 October supply will be cut in parts of Beacon Valley, Bellville South, Mfuleni, Kommetjie, and Bloekombos and Wallacedene, with tankers in Beacon Valley and Mfuleni. Parts of Woodstock and all of Schaapkraal in Philippi face test shut-offs early in the week and a full shut-off from 09:00 to 18:00 on Thursday 29 October. The City advises storing water in clean, sealed containers beforehand and keeping taps closed until supply returns; the full list, including low-pressure testing in Tamboerskloof, Gardens and Khayelitsha, is on its website.
{: #water-shutoffs }

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/planned-water-supply-maintenance-from-24-to-31-october/)

**Duynefontein, Van Riebeeckstrand, Kleinzouterivier, Melkbosstrand and surrounding areas will have no electricity from about 10:00 to 11:00 on Tuesday 27 October while Eskom does maintenance.** The City says the times are an estimate and restoration may take longer. It asks residents to treat all installations as live during the work and to switch off appliances to protect them from surges. Queries go to the City's call centre on 0860 103 089.

## 🚨 Safety

**The Western Cape Government says it will investigate taxi associations and operators linked to a recent run of shootings in Somerset West, Khayelitsha, Epping, Delft, Bonteheuwel and Maitland.** Mobility Minister Isaac Sileku said some of the violence is suspected to stem from rival organisations fighting over routes, and that the province will not mediate where operators have taken routes without legal authority. The Registrar's office will check whether associations broke their own rules, the Provincial Regulatory Entity will review the licences of operators implicated, and the department plans urgent court orders against organisations encroaching on other councils' routes. It said information it gave SAPS about two minibus taxis linked to earlier shootings has led to two arrests. In Delft, two people were killed, one of them a bystander, Cape Town ETC reports.
{: #taxi-violence }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/western-cape-government-steps-up-action-against-taxi-violence/)

## 🏛️ Local government

**Residents of three Khayelitsha informal settlements blocked Baden Powell Drive and Japhta Masemola Road with burning tyres for about three hours on Friday morning, 23 October, demanding water and electricity.** The protesters came from Ethembeni, Empolweni and Island. A community leader told GroundUp that about 450 families in Ethembeni rely on four taps that have never worked reliably since people settled there in 2020, and that residents could not put out a fire that destroyed two shacks two weeks ago. Residents said they pay R10 for 20 litres of water and pay people to make illegal electricity connections, which the community leader said have caused shack fires. They said meetings with City water officials had produced nothing. Law enforcement officers rerouted traffic; GroundUp said it had asked the City and Eskom for comment and had not yet received replies.
{: #khayelitsha-protest data-place="Baden Powell Drive, Khayelitsha" }

> [GroundUp](https://groundup.org.za/article/protesters-block-major-cape-town-highway-demand-water-electricity/)

**The City says it has dealt with 85% of the backlog of trenches, pavements and driveways left unrepaired after water and sewer work in 2025/26, with 2,335 still outstanding.** For 2024/25, 97% are done and 375 remain open, and the City has increased the number of staff checking reported sites from four to ten. Water and Sanitation Mayco member Zahid Badroodien said the backlog cannot be cleared overnight. Work is planned in more than 40 areas in the coming weeks, including Woodstock, Langa, Strand, Mfuleni and Table View. If a temporary repair becomes unsafe, residents can email water.escalations@capetown.gov.za with the service request number and a photo.
{: #trench-reinstatements }

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/city-steps-up-road-and-pavement-reinstatements-as-backlog-declines/)

## 🗳️ Elections 2026

**Two surveys have given very different readings of support for the City's planned N2 Edge safety project, the barrier along the N2.** An eNCA-MarkData election survey found almost 70% of respondents in favour and about 20% against, while a separate report found nearly 94% opposed, Cape Town ETC reports; it notes the results cannot be compared without sample sizes and methods. The project, tabled by Mayor Geordin Hill-Lewis in January, includes repairing barriers between Baden Powell Drive and Bhunga Avenue, better lighting and crossings, at a reported R114 million. GOOD's mayoral candidate Brett Herron said the money should be weighed against housing, sports facilities and neighbourhood upgrades, and a National Coloured Congress representative called for policing resources to go to areas affected by gang violence.

## 🏠 Housing & development

**Twenty-six more families received the keys to new homes at the Florida site of the Elsies River infill housing project on Thursday 23 October.** The City project is due to deliver 724 homes on six sites in Elsies River, the largest with 303 homes, with beneficiaries chosen from the housing needs register. Human Settlements Mayco member Carl Pophaim warned against intimidation and attempts to interfere with allocations, without saying what disruptions the project has faced. Suspected interference can be reported on 021 480 7700.

## 🌊 Environment

**The dams supplying Cape Town were 73.8% full this week, slightly down on last week's 74.0% and well below 89.7% at the same time last year.** Voëlvlei, one of the larger dams, is at 56.1% against 99.3% a year ago, and Steenbras Lower at 47.7% against 93.3%. Theewaterskloof, the biggest, is at 73.7%, while Berg River (93.9%) and Steenbras Upper (99.4%) are close to full.
