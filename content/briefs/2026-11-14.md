## 🚨 Safety

**Two Metro Police officers and two civilians were injured shortly after 06:00 on Friday 13 November when a passing car hit them on the inbound N1 just past the Koeberg Interchange, Cape Town ETC reports.** The officers had stopped to help the occupants of a broken-down vehicle. Both officers and one civilian were taken to hospital. Metro Police spokesperson Ruth Solomons said the driver tested negative for alcohol and that a case of reckless and negligent driving would be registered; the investigation is continuing. Slow down and move over when you pass emergency vehicles or a breakdown on the shoulder.
{: #n1-crash data-place="Koeberg Interchange" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/four-injured-after-vehicle-ploughs-into-roadside-breakdown-scene-on-cape-towns-n1/)

**A 46-year-old man was arrested in Kensington at about 03:30 on Friday 13 November over text messages sent on 17 October that allegedly threatened Western Cape Premier Alan Winde and another person, police said, Cape Town ETC reports.** Police are also investigating an alleged direct threat to shoot the person who received the messages. The provincial Murder and Robbery Unit and Crime Intelligence traced the man and seized a cellphone believed to have been used. He faces charges of intimidation and contravening the Cybercrimes Act, and was expected in the Cape Town Magistrate's Court on Friday.
{: #winde-threats data-place="Kensington" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/cape-town-man-arrested-over-alleged-threats-to-shoot-complainant-and-premier-alan-winde/)

**The 53-year-old man accused of killing his 88-year-old father at their Eversdal home on Tuesday 10 November appeared in the Bellville Magistrate's Court on Friday 13 November, where the State said it would oppose bail, Cape Town ETC reports.** The case was postponed to Tuesday 17 November for a district surgeon's report, and he remains in custody. His lawyer told the court he has no previous convictions and a history of brain injury and psychosis, and said a bail application would follow the assessment.
{: #eversdal data-place="Eversdal Road, Stellenberg" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/eversdal-community-shaken-after-88-year-old-man-allegedly-killed-by-son/)

## 🚌 Getting around

**Another truck got stuck under the Atlantic Road railway bridge in Muizenberg on Friday afternoon, 13 November, causing delays on Atlantic Road, Cape Town ETC reports.** It follows a similar strike on Monday 9 November at the low bridge, which locals call "Bridget". Drivers of tall vehicles should check their height before using the road, and other motorists should expect delays or use another route while traffic measures are in place.
{: #muizenberg-bridge data-place="Atlantic Road, Muizenberg" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/bridgets-appetite-strikes-again-expect-delays-in-muizenberg/)

**The City's CCTV network recorded 246 smash-and-grab incidents in the 2025/26 financial year, and reports to the City and community police forums point to several hotspots, Cape Town ETC reports.** They include Jakes Gerwel Drive between the N2 and Bofors Circle, Epping Avenue and Jan van Riebeeck Drive around Elsies River, and intersections on Voortrekker Road and Jan van Riebeeck Drive in Parow. Keep valuables out of sight and stay alert at traffic lights.
{: #smash-and-grab }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/cape-town-smash-and-grab-hotspots-motorists-should-know/)

## 🌊 Environment

**Two baboon troops from Waterfall and Seaforth, near Simon's Town, are being moved to the Urban Wildlife Centre at Kogelfontein under a permit, with a Cape of Good Hope SPCA inspector monitoring their welfare, Cape Town ETC reports.** The relocation began on Friday 13 November after members of the public raised concerns. The SPCA said its presence does not mean it supports placing healthy, free-ranging baboons in permanent captivity, but that it accepts a sanctuary as a way to protect them from immediate harm, and that the centre must meet the troops' long-term needs. The decision rests with the authorities.
{: #baboons data-place="Simon's Town" }
