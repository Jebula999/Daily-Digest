## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**The Glencairn Expressway was still closed on Saturday evening, 14 November, after a fire in Capri earlier that day, Cape Town ETC reports.** Motorists were advised to use Kommetjie Road through Fish Hoek or Slangkop Road. Deep Blue Security asked people who don't live in Capri to stay away, and asked residents to move parked cars off the roads so firefighters can get through and to keep pets indoors. No damage or injuries had been reported. Check for updates before driving through the Deep South this morning.
{: #capri-fire data-place="Glencairn Expressway" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/glencairn-expressway-still-closed-after-capri-fire/)

**The City has scheduled water maintenance across several suburbs this week, the biggest being a shutdown of the Faure Water Treatment Plant from 07:00 on Wednesday 18 November to 18:00 on Thursday 19 November, Cape Town ETC reports.** Water will be redirected from the Steenbras and Blackheath pipelines during the Faure shutdown, and residents and businesses are asked to cut their use. On Thursday 19 November, from 09:00 to 19:00, Woodstock, Salt River and Observatory may be without water while a leaking bulk main in Sir Lowry Road is repaired; water tankers will be available where needed. Water will also be off in parts of Lotus River (Lake Road, Third to Fifth Avenue) and Grassy Park (Eighth Avenue, Yorkton, Anne and Iris roads) on Tuesday 17 November from 09:00 to 18:00, and in Welgedacht, Selborne Slot, Oude Westhof, Van Riebeeckshof and Kanonberg from 20:00 on Tuesday to 05:00 on Wednesday. There is a test shutdown in Kuils River Industria, Brantwood, De Kuilen and Letru on Tuesday, and pressure testing that may cause low pressure or brief cuts in Welcome Glen and Da Gama Park (Monday), Diep River (Tuesday), Kommetjie (Wednesday), and Retreat, Steenberg, Cafda Village, Coniston Park and Sheraton Park (Thursday), all from 09:00 to 16:00. Store some water before work in your area.
{: #water-work }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/cape-town-water-disruptions-affected-areas-from-14-20-november/)

## 🚨 Safety

**The NSRI has asked Capetonians to help protect its pink rescue buoys after several went missing or were stolen from their stands, Cape Town ETC reports.** The buoys are there so bystanders can help a struggling swimmer until rescuers arrive. More than 2,100 have been installed around the country, and the NSRI says they have helped save 259 lives. Its Station 14 has replaced as many missing buoys as it can. If you notice a buoy missing from its stand, or see anyone tampering with one, report it to the NSRI.
{: #rescue-buoys }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/nsri-urges-cape-town-residents-to-protect-pink-rescue-buoys/)

**The City's fire service has deployed extra equipment ahead of the fire season, Cape Town ETC reports.** The additions include 10 water tankers, eight off-road skid units, two rescue vehicles for crashes and technical rescues, a major-pumper fire engine, an all-terrain Bronto unit and three turntable ladder trucks for high-rise emergencies. The City has 32 fire stations.
{: #fire-resources }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/cape-town-boosts-firefighting-resources/)

## 🏠 Housing & development

**The City's upgrade of the Muizenberg beachfront is almost 90% complete, and most construction should be finished by mid-December, Cape Town ETC reports.** From 16 December the whole beach, all the parking, the new promenade and stepped seawall from Surfer's Corner towards the Pavilion, and the beach huts should be open, with new showers expected to be working. The new central toilets may also open, but this is not yet confirmed; the temporary toilets on the western side will stay. Mosaics, some paving, pergolas, the eastern lawn and the remaining seating walls will be finished in the new year. Work started in February 2025.
{: #muizenberg-beachfront data-place="Muizenberg Beach" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/muizenberg-beachfront-upgrade-december-2026/)

## 🩺 Health & community

**Western Cape Social Development Minister Wendy Kaizer-Philander has questioned why none of the R147.2m set aside to fill 228 SASSA vacancies has been spent, Cape Town ETC reports.** According to a parliamentary reply, 144 of the posts are in local and district offices. After visiting SASSA's Mitchells Plain office, the minister said staff shortages there add to delays and long queues. She wants funded posts filled immediately and faster hiring by regional offices. SASSA has said it will fill local office posts as they become vacant.
