## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Water is off in parts of Woodstock from 09:00 to 18:00 today, Tuesday 24 November, for planned City maintenance.** Affected streets include Victoria, Pine, Salmon, Warwick, Queens, Woodlands and Ravenscraig roads. Hanover Park has low pressure from 09:00 to 14:00, and zero-pressure tests, which can mean low pressure or no water, are planned in Protea Park and Wesfleur in Atlantis. The City advises storing water in clean, sealed containers beforehand and keeping taps closed during the shutdown.
{: #woodstock-water data-place="Woodstock" }

> [City of Cape Town](https://www.capetown.gov.za/media-and-news/planned-water-supply-maintenance-from-23-to-29-november-2026/), [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/cape-town-water-disruptions-affected-areas-and-dates-in-november/)

**Water supply to parts of Boulders in Table Mountain National Park is disrupted, affecting the visitor centre and the middle toilet block, SANParks said on Monday 23 November, according to Cape Town ETC.** SANParks said the City has been told and is dealing with the problem, but there is no time yet for when supply will be restored. Visitors should expect those facilities may not be available as usual.

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/boulders-water-supply-disruption/)

## 🚨 Safety

**The City's enforcement agencies made 133 drunk-driving arrests in the past week, the City said on Monday 23 November, after a driver crashed into a Metro Police vehicle on the N2.** At about 00:30 on Monday, a Volkswagen travelling at high speed towards Somerset West hit the roadside barrier and then the front of a marked Metro Police patrol car. The driver's breathalyser reading was more than twice the legal limit, and he was arrested for drunk driving and reckless and negligent driving; one officer had minor knee injuries. Over the week, traffic officers also executed 2,727 warrants, impounded 116 public transport vehicles and issued 57,228 fines, and the City's emergency communication centre logged 3,381 incidents, including 91 domestic violence cases. Emergencies and suspicious activity can be reported to the centre on 021 480 7700.

## 🚌 Getting around

**Firdale Avenue and Leeuwendal Crescent in Gardens are closed to through traffic from Monday 23 November until Tuesday 15 December while the City rebuilds both roads, Cape Town ETC reports.** The City's Urban Mobility Directorate said Firdale Avenue is too narrow to keep open during the work, which includes new road surfaces or paving blocks, pavement repairs and new kerbs and channels. Only residents may drive in; other traffic is sent via Kloof Nek Road and Constantia Road, and drivers should expect delays. Access to properties will be kept open where possible, and vehicles that block the works may be moved or towed.
{: #gardens-roads data-place="Firdale Avenue, Gardens" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/two-gardens-roads-to-close-for-rehabilitation-works-until-mid-december/)

## 🩺 Health & community

**Beacon School in Westridge, Mitchells Plain, is the only special needs school in the Western Cape taking part in robotics competitions, and its learners reached the national World Robot Olympiad in both 2025 and 2026, GroundUp reports.** The school teaches learners with severe intellectual disabilities and autism. Teacher Zelia van Rooyen started with LEGO kits sponsored in 2024 and moved into coding after training; the school's teams placed first and second at the provincial round in Paarl before competing at the nationals in Pretoria. Cape Town is due to host the World Robot Olympiad international final in 2028.
