## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Buitengracht Street in the city centre has a water shut-off today, Wednesday 11 November, from 09:00 to 18:30, between Military Road and Leeuwen Street, while a new meter is connected, Cape Town ETC reports.** Network testing from 09:00 to 16:00 could also interrupt supply in areas including Diep River, Wynberg, Tamboerskloof and Gardens. On Thursday 12 November, part of Plumstead (around Morton, Tiverton and Exeter Roads) is without water from 09:00 to 19:30, Steenberg roads including Lake and Regis Roads from 08:00 to 20:00, and Rondebosch East, Kenwyn, Rylands and parts of Lansdowne will have low pressure from 09:00 to 17:00. Store water beforehand and keep taps closed during the work.

## 🚨 Safety

**Two women were killed when they were reportedly struck by a City of Cape Town Metro Police vehicle on Lansdowne Road in Khayelitsha at about 18:30 on Sunday 8 November, Cape Town ETC reports.** Both were pedestrians and were declared dead at the scene. Khayelitsha police have opened a culpable homicide case, the Independent Police Investigative Directorate (IPID) is investigating with SAPS, and the City has started its own internal investigation. Metro Police spokesperson Ruth Solomons said the Safety and Security Directorate is cooperating fully. No arrests had been made. Anyone with information can call Crime Stop on 08600 10111.
{: #khayelitsha-crash data-place="Lansdowne Road, Khayelitsha" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/khayelitsha-metro-police-accident-two-women-killed/)

**Firefighters contained a vegetation fire on the lower slopes of Signal Hill shortly before 14:00 on Tuesday 10 November, the third fire in the area in two weeks, Cape Town ETC reports.** The fire started outside the national park and spread into it at about 13:15. Table Mountain National Park sent 55 firefighters and five vehicles, and crews stayed on to put out hotspots. Earlier fires were fought on 28 October and 9 November. The cause has not been given. Report smoke immediately: the fire season runs from October to May.
{: #signal-hill data-place="Signal Hill" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/firefighters-tackle-another-fire-on-cape-towns-signal-hill/)

**The High Court in Cape Town has sentenced a 41-year-old man to an effective 30 years in prison for the 2023 murder of Ses'Khona activist Loyiso Nkohla-Mabandla, SABC News reports.** He pleaded guilty to 15 charges under a plea and sentencing agreement. Nkohla-Mabandla was shot dead in Philippi in April 2023 while mediating the relocation of people living in shacks on PRASA's Central line. Two other accused will appear separately.
{: #nkohla-sentence }

> [SABC News](https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/loyiso-nkohla-mabandla-murder-man-sentenced-to-30-years-in-prison/)

**A 44-year-old man was arrested after about R449,000 in cash was stolen from an unlocked vehicle in Section Street, Paarden Eiland, on Monday 9 November, police said.** The businessman had stopped on the way to the bank. Police said they found three signal-jamming devices and cash the man could not account for during a search in Watergate, Mitchells Plain. He is due in the Mitchells Plain Magistrate's Court once charged.

## 🏛️ Local government

**Political parties have until Wednesday 18 November to remove election posters and banners in Cape Town, the City said on Tuesday, The Citizen reports.** Posters still up after the deadline will be removed by the City at the party's cost, at R123.48 each excluding VAT. If they remain after a formal notice, the City will charge a penalty of R500 per poster. Residents can report posters left up after 18 November.

## 🏠 Housing & development

**Families in YB section, Khayelitsha, are using buckets as toilets and emptying waste into stormwater drains because a communal toilet block has been vandalised and left blocked, GroundUp reports.** The section has 338 households and 51 flush toilets, ten of them in the broken block. Water and Sanitation mayoral committee member Zahid Badroodien said only the bowls remain, and that the City is looking at repairs and considering chemical toilets in the meantime, subject to budget. He said a delayed janitorial programme had held up the hiring of cleaners.

## 🌊 Environment

**The City's R430 million upgrade of the Sir Lowry's Pass River to cut flood risk in Gordon's Bay is about 82% complete and on track to finish at the end of 2027, the City said, Cape Town ETC reports.** Work since February 2023 has reshaped the river between the N2 and the Gordon's Bay wastewater plant and built a diversion canal to the St Andrews Drive bridge. The City said flooding has already eased on Dennehof Road and at Fairview Golf Estate. Drop structures and the last canal sections remain.
