## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**Water is off in several areas today, Wednesday 25 November, for planned City maintenance and an emergency pipeline repair.** Canal Road in Wetton is without water from 08:00 to 20:00, and Dreyer Street and surrounding roads in Claremont from 09:00 to 18:00. Parts of Schaapkraal have no water from 08:00 to 18:00, and a leaking valve on a bulk pipeline in Papkuilsvlei Road, Philippi, is being repaired from 09:00 to midnight, which may also lower pressure in Strandfontein. Pressure tests, which can mean low pressure or no water, run in Montclair, Brooklyn, Rugby, Tijgerhof, Sanddrift, Century City, Milnerton, Summer Greens, Tamboerskloof and Gardens. On Thursday 26 November, Brackenfell and Brackenfell South, including Vredekloof, Arauna, Morgenster, St Kilda, Eden Park and Okavango Park, are without water from 10:00 to 18:00. The City advises storing water in clean, sealed containers beforehand.

## 🚨 Safety

**The Cape Town Central City Improvement District (CCID) and Cape Town Tourism have launched a campaign warning visitors about a card scam in which criminals pose as officials and ask for a "free permit" to be paid on a card machine, Cape Town ETC reports.** The scammers typically claim a street is closed for a film shoot, a presidential visit or security reasons, then take the victim's bank card and PIN and use the card to withdraw money. The campaign runs in seven languages through hotels, guesthouses and tour operators, with a focus on December. The CCID said card fraud in the CBD has dropped significantly over the past year. The basic rule: never hand your card or PIN to anyone claiming to issue a permit.
{: #card-scam data-place="Cape Town city centre" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/cape-town-tourists-warned-about-credit-card-scam-ahead-of-festive-season/)

**Two men accused of the shooting at a shebeen in Marikana informal settlement, Philippi, on 17 January, in which eight people were killed and two injured, appeared in the Athlone Magistrates' Court on Monday 23 November, where their case was transferred to the Western Cape High Court for trial, GroundUp reports.** Both remain in custody at Pollsmoor. Police issued a warrant in February for a third suspect, aged 28, who is still being sought; provincial police spokesperson Ndakhe Gwala said on Tuesday that the investigation is ongoing.

## 🏛️ Local government

**The Western Cape's economy grew 1.4% in 2025 and the province added 579,000 jobs over five years, but rising living costs are squeezing households, Finance MEC Deidré Baartman said on Monday 23 November when she tabled the province's economic and socio-economic outlook reports.** Unemployment stood at 19.5% in the second quarter of 2026, against 33.6% nationally. The reports say fuel prices have risen about 55% and food 33% since 2021, and the share of residents living in poverty rose from 37.8% in 2023 to 39.7% in 2025. Baartman said the country has become "too expensive for even a middle-class family". The provincial Medium-Term Budget Policy Statement is due in the provincial legislature tomorrow, Thursday 26 November.

## 🩺 Health & community

**Western Cape matric candidates write their last National Senior Certificate paper, Music Paper 2, today, Wednesday 25 November, ending seven weeks of exams, the Western Cape Education Department said.** The 71,302 full-time candidates wrote 190 question papers over 28 exam days. About 4,675 markers will now mark more than a million scripts at 13 marking centres in the province. National results are announced on Monday 11 January 2027, and candidates can collect their results at school or on the WCED website from Tuesday 12 January.
{: #matric-ends }

> [Western Cape Education Department](https://www.westerncape.gov.za/education/article/pens-down-classof2026)

**The Western Cape recorded 1,565 cases of diarrhoea in children in the first two weeks of November, down from 2,257 in the same period last year, Cape Town ETC reports, citing VOC News.** The provincial health department is treating the drop with caution because hospital admission figures are incomplete. Infants can become dehydrated within hours. Parents should give small, frequent sips of oral rehydration solution (the recipe is on page 30 of the Road to Health booklet) and get medical help quickly if a baby is listless, a child is vomiting everything, or there are convulsions.

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/health-and-wellness/summer-gastro-season-parents-know-signs/)
