## 🗳️ Elections 2026

**Voting stations are open today, Wednesday 4 November, from 07:00 to 21:00 for the local government elections, and the day is a public holiday.** Vote at the station where you are registered and bring a valid South African identity document. Photographing a marked ballot is not allowed. Over the two special voting days, 1,425,942 people voted nationally, 148,493 of them in the Western Cape, the IEC said. Special votes are stored securely and will be counted with today's ballots after stations close tonight. A record 29.2 million people are registered to vote. To check your voting station, SMS your ID number to 32810 or send "Hi" to the IEC on WhatsApp at 060 088 0000.

**Police arrested a 32-year-old man described as a wanted gang member after shots were fired at officers on Ruimte Road in Manenberg, about 150 metres from a voting station, police said on Tuesday 3 November.** Members of the SAPS Anti-Gang Unit deployed for the election period say the man opened fire when he noticed them, and they returned fire. Nobody was reported injured. Police recovered a firearm with an extended magazine and 32 rounds of ammunition. He is expected to appear in court on charges of attempted murder and possession of an unlicensed firearm.

## 🚨 Safety

**Guy Fawkes is tomorrow, Thursday 5 November, and the City says there are no designated sites for setting off fireworks, after an eight-year-old boy in Manenberg died on Sunday 1 November following a reported firecracker incident.** Police have opened an inquest, and a post-mortem will establish the cause of death. The community group FACSA has called for more enforcement against the sale of firecrackers to children. The City's emergency centre logged at least 49 complaints about illegal fireworks use and 13 about illegal sales in the first three weeks of October, up from nine complaints in all in September; hotspots included Manenberg, Hanover Park and Bonteheuwel. Displays need SAPS and City permission, and fines include R300 for selling fireworks to, or letting, anyone under 16 handle them unsupervised, and R1,500 for selling without a certificate. Report illegal sales or use on 021 480 7700 or through the City app.
{: #guy-fawkes data-place="Manenberg" }

**A one-year-old child drowned on Saturday 31 October after falling into an open municipal drain on Hintsaka Pahlo Street in Samora Machel, GroundUp reports.** Water and sanitation mayoral committee member Zahid Badroodien confirmed the death, said police are investigating and that the City is co-operating, and declined to answer questions about the contractor. The community group Movement for CARE said the drain had been open since August, when work on a sewer project began, and that the site should have been watched at all times. When GroundUp visited on Monday, the site was cordoned off and workers were filling the hole.

**The Law Enforcement Advancement Plan (LEAP), the City and provincial programme of more than 1,000 officers in high-crime areas, has confiscated 1,003 illegal firearms and 13,362 rounds of ammunition since it began in February 2020, the City said.** The seizures led to 1,133 arrests, and Delft recorded the most confiscations. Safety and security mayoral committee member JP Smith questioned whether the arrests are leading to convictions, and pointed to a bail decision in Nyanga in a case involving four rifles.

## 🩺 Health & community

**South Africa has had 26 confirmed mpox cases this year, 18 of them in the Western Cape, and 20 of the 26 were found after August, according to Spotlight in Daily Maverick.** No deaths have been reported this year, and seven people were admitted to hospital. The national health department has 3,865 donated vaccine doses left, for people at high risk and for those exposed to a known case, and says they will be used as needed. Symptoms include fever, headache, swollen lymph nodes and a painful rash; anyone with an unexplained rash should get medical advice and avoid close contact while waiting for a test.
{: #mpox }

> [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-11-03-mpox-vaccine-stock-will-be-deployed-as-needed-as-cases-climb-to-26/)

**Twelve Western Cape primary schools, including Downeville Primary in Manenberg, Oklahomastraat Primêre in Macassar and Du Noon Primary, will get fenced, solar-lit multi-purpose sport courts in early 2027.** Each court costs more than R2 million and is part of a R130 million, three-year provincial school sport strategy, ministers Ricardo Mackenzie and Jaco Londt announced on Monday 2 November. The schools were chosen from 77 applications.
