## ⚠️ Alerts & outages

**The City of Cape Town has asked residents, businesses and visitors to use water sparingly, saying households need to cut consumption by at least 10% through summer and into next winter, SAnews reports.** Daily use recently reached 912 million litres, more than 52 million litres above the target of 860 million, and dam levels stand at 71.7%, on average about 15% lower than a year ago. The City expects a summer that is longer, hotter and drier than usual. No restrictions are in place; the national Department of Water and Sanitation's annual assessment will decide whether any are needed for the Western Cape supply system. The City's suggestions include shorter showers, running washing machines and dishwashers only when full, covering pools, and watering gardens for no more than an hour before 09:00 or after 18:00.

**Planned water work continues today, Thursday 12 November: part of Plumstead around Morton, Tiverton and Exeter Roads is without water from 09:00 to 19:30, and roads in Steenberg including Lake and Regis Roads from 08:00 to 20:00, Cape Town ETC reports.** Rondebosch East, Kenwyn, Rylands and parts of Lansdowne can expect low pressure from 09:00 to 17:00.

## 🚨 Safety

**Three people were shot dead in Khayelitsha in two separate incidents linked to taxi violence, police said on Wednesday 11 November, SABC News reports.** In one, two men aged 35 and 36 died at the scene and a third man, also 35, was seriously wounded. In the other, a taxi driver was shot dead at his home in Harare, Khayelitsha. Police spokesperson Thembakazi Mpendukana said no arrests had been made. The Western Cape spokesperson for the taxi council Santaco, Makhosandile Tumana, called for calm, saying the violence was continuing despite an interim court interdict and talks with the authorities. A five-hour meeting on Tuesday agreed that only taxis with valid permits may run on the disputed Delft–Mutual and Delft–Epping routes. The interdict returns to court on 20 November.
{: #taxi-violence data-place="Khayelitsha" }

> [SABC News: shootings](https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/three-killed-in-separate-taxi-violence-shootings-in-khayelitsha/), [SABC News: Santaco](https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/santaco-calls-for-calm-amid-taxi-violence-in-the-western-cape/)

**An 88-year-old man died after he was allegedly assaulted at a home on Eversdal Road in Stellenberg, Bellville, on Tuesday evening, 10 November, and his 53-year-old son has been arrested for murder, Cape Town ETC reports.** Police spokesperson Ndakhe Gwala confirmed the arrest and said the circumstances were still being investigated. Paramedics who were called at about 19:15 found no signs of life. The son is expected to appear in the Bellville Magistrate's Court.
{: #eversdal data-place="Eversdal Road, Stellenberg" }

> [Cape Town ETC](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/eversdal-community-shaken-after-88-year-old-man-allegedly-killed-by-son/)

**The Cape Town Regional Court has sentenced a 42-year-old former accountant at a Cape Town travel company to 15 years in prison for stealing about R17.4 million from her employer, Cape Town ETC reports.** She was convicted on 134 counts of theft and money laundering for taking the money between January 2019 and June 2024. The company was not named.

## 🚌 Getting around

**Motorists using roads around Nyanga, including the N2 near Cape Town International Airport and the Borcherds Quarry Road intersections, are being urged to stay alert after reports of criminals throwing objects at cars or putting stones and spikes on the road to force drivers to stop, Cape Town ETC reports.** Once a car stops, suspects try to steal valuables, and others move between cars in slow traffic looking for phones and bags in view. Take extra care near pedestrian bridges and embankments, especially in peak-hour traffic, and keep valuables out of sight.
