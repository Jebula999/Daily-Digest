## 🚌 Getting around

**Petrol prices are on track to fall by R3.44 a litre for 93 octane and R3.68 for 95 in December, according to Central Energy Fund figures at the end of the third week of November reported by Cape Town ETC.** Diesel shows an over-recovery of R1.58 to R2.00 a litre, depending on the grade. The figures are not final and do not yet include the slate levy or other monthly adjustments, and part of Treasury's temporary fuel-levy relief is being reversed: 50c a litre goes back onto petrol and R1.50 onto diesel from December, with the rest falling away in January. The main driver is lower international oil prices, at about $91 a barrel after topping $100 for much of the past month. The Department of Mineral and Petroleum Resources will confirm the official prices early in December.

## 🌊 Environment

**A veldfire burning in the De Hoop Nature Reserve and on Potberg mountain in the Overberg reached the Riverine residential complex near Malgas, where about 15 houses were reported burning, Overberg District Municipality fire chief Reinard Geldenhuys said, according to Cape Town ETC.** No injuries had been reported, and authorities were looking into one possible missing-person case, which was unconfirmed. In an update at 21:00 on Friday 20 November, the Greater Overberg Fire Protection Association said the fire was 40% contained, up from none on Thursday, but still active on Potberg and in the reserve, with spot fires and flare-ups and a further wind change expected on Saturday. CapeNature, Volunteer Wildfire Services and the Working on Fire programme are among the teams on the fire lines.

## 🩺 Health & community

**The South African Bail Fund, which pays bail of R1,000 or less for detainees who cannot afford it, officially launched in Cape Town on Friday 20 November after a pilot at Pollsmoor and Goodwood prisons that has helped free 330 people since May, GroundUp reports.** The pilot was started by retired Constitutional Court judge Edwin Cameron, a former inspecting judge of prisons, and the fund is paid for by donors including the Millennium Trust, the Bertha Foundation and the Claude Leon Foundation. Director Jody-Lee Fredericks said about a third of those helped were charged with drug possession, and that many of the relatives the fund contacted were pensioners or could not afford bail themselves. Holding a remand detainee costs the state about R463 a day, and according to a parliamentary reply in March, Western Cape prisons are 70% over capacity, with Pollsmoor's Remand Detention Facility A at 308%. Correctional Services Minister Pieter Groenewald attended the launch.
