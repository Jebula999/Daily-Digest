## 🚨 Safety

**Murders fell by 30.6% in Mitchells Plain, 23.2% in Gugulethu and 22.6% in Philippi East in the second quarter of the 2026/27 financial year (July to September), according to a Western Cape Government statement issued on Friday 20 November and reported by Cape Town ETC.** All three areas have officers deployed under LEAP, the Law Enforcement Advancement Plan run jointly by the provincial government and the City, and the statement says murders have now fallen for four quarters in a row, starting with October to December 2025. It did not give the number of murders in each area. Premier Alan Winde said the reductions show "progress is possible", but that much more needs to be done, and called for municipalities to be given more control over policing resources. Community Safety Minister Anroux Marais said a provincial safety and development programme is being piloted in Mitchells Plain, and called for more work to shut down firearm and ammunition supply networks.

**Four men were killed and six other people injured when gunmen opened fire on patrons at a tavern on Ihobe Street, Khayelitsha, on Saturday night, 21 November, police told Cape Town ETC.** Police spokesperson Brigadier Novela Potelwa said the attackers fled in a vehicle they hijacked from people at the tavern, which was later found abandoned a few kilometres away. The provincial Serious and Violent Crime unit is investigating murder and attempted murder cases. No arrests have been made and the motive is not known. Anyone with information can call Crime Stop on 08600 10111 or use the MySAPS app.

## 🚌 Getting around

**Roads around the Grand Parade will close for the City's Festive Lights Switch-On on Sunday 29 November, with one lane of Darling Street between Parade and Corporation streets closed from tomorrow, Tuesday 24 November, until Tuesday 1 December for stage building, Cape Town ETC reports.** Further closures on Darling, Adderley, Longmarket and surrounding streets apply from 05:00 on Sunday to 05:00 on Monday 30 November, and stopping is banned on Parade, Adderley, Parliament, Corporation, Buitenkant, Church, Longmarket and Barrack streets from 16:00 on Saturday 28 November to midnight on Monday. On Sunday night MyCiTi's D01 to Khayelitsha runs until 23:00, the last D02 to Kuyasa and D04 to Kapteinsklip leave at 23:00, and the T01 to Dunoon runs until 22:30; route 106 is diverted via Strand Street and will not stop at Groote Kerk. E-hailing pick-up points are on Darling Street outside the Castle of Good Hope and at Strand and Adderley streets. The free concert runs from 16:00 to 22:00, and alcohol, umbrellas, gazebos and gas sprays are among the items not allowed.

## 🩺 Health & community

**American runner Careth Arnold set a women's course record of 23:53:08 in the 100-mile race at the Ultra-Trail Cape Town on Saturday 21 November, taking more than 47 minutes off the previous best, Cape Town ETC reports.** Arnold finished third overall in the race, which started on Friday evening at Gardens Rugby Club and crossed Table Mountain National Park and the Cape Peninsula. Aleksei Tolstenko, running as a neutral athlete, won the men's 100 miles in 21:32:48 for his third title. South Africa's Kerry-Ann Marshall was third among women and became the first athlete to run more than 1,000 km across UTCT races.

**Cape Town will host the World Robot Olympiad International Final in 2028, the first time the youth robotics competition has been held in Africa, GroundUp reports.** The competition runs in more than 95 countries, and the number of South African teams has grown from about 40 when it started here in 2010 to about 1,800 in 2025. GroundUp spoke to former competitors now studying engineering, computer science and medicine at Stellenbosch, UWC and UCT.
