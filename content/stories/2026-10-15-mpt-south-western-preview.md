The City's **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** for the south-western district meets online on **Tuesday 20 October at 08:30**. The tribunal decides land-use applications, such as rezonings, subdivisions and departures from building rules, that are too large or too contested for officials to approve on their own.

## What's on the agenda

**Southern suburbs (Ward 62 and neighbours)**

- **Constantia:** five Ward 62 applications. They include a rezoning and subdivision at Glendirk Farm on Klaassens Road; a combined rezoning, consolidation and subdivision with a site development plan at 6 Duntaw Close and 8 Bel Ombre Road; a subdivision at 7 Evergreen Lane; and changes to title-deed conditions so land can be subdivided at 109 Southern Cross Drive.
- **Newlands:** deleting title-deed restrictions so a property at 57 Rhodes Avenue can be subdivided.
- **Rondebosch (Ward 59):** a rezoning at 56 Dean Street that also needs council approval.
- **Hout Bay (Ward 74):** a subdivision with a consent use and departure at 4A Breton Close.
- **Southfield (Ward 72):** a subdivision and departures at 173 Princess Vlei Road.
- **Zeekoevlei:** a permanent departure at 17 Fish Road.

**Cape Flats**

- **Philippi (Ward 43):** subdivision, consolidation, consent use and departures across portions of two Cape Farms at **101 Ottery Road**, the largest application on the agenda by land area.
- **Gugulethu (Ward 39):** a consent use and departure at 96 Mpinga Street.

**Penalties for unauthorised building**

Sixteen properties face administrative penalties, which owners must pay before work done without approval can be legalised. They are in Constantia, Hout Bay, Strandfontein (two), Lansdowne, Surrey Estate (two), Wetton, Lotus River (two), Highlands Estate, Bay View in Mitchells Plain, Athlone (two), Knole Park and Schaapkraal.

## How to follow it

The agenda and, after the meeting, the resolutions are published on the City's [Council Hub](https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/councilhubonline/councilandCommitteemeetings) under *Municipal Planning Tribunal*. Applicants and people who formally objected during the comment period are normally notified and may address the tribunal. We'll report the outcomes once the resolutions are published.
