The **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** is the City panel that decides rezonings, subdivisions and departures from building rules, and sets penalties for building without approval. Its south-western branch, covering the southern suburbs, the far south and much of the Cape Flats, met online on Tuesday 17 November ([what was on the agenda](/stories/2026-11-11-mpt-south-western-preview/)). Every decision recorded was unanimous, except the Gugulethu relocation area, where no vote count is given. In each case the tribunal said it followed the reasons in the planner's report.

## Refused

- **4 Feldhausen Road, Claremont (Ward 59):** a rezoning from General Residential GR4 to GR5, which allows taller and denser residential buildings, and the departures that went with it.
- **2 Lyle Road, Rosebank (Ward 57):** a subdivision and departures.

## Approved

- **15 Vukuzenzele Street, Gugulethu (Ward 44):** a temporary land-use departure for a Temporary Relocation Area, a site where residents are housed while they wait for permanent homes, in line with a site layout plan dated 10 July 2026.
- **Erf 6482, Grassy Park (Ward 66):** a deviation from the Cape Flats District Plan, rezoning from community zoning to a subdivisional area overlay, and subdivision, built out in phases. The overlay allows a mix of housing, community facilities, roads, open space and utilities on the site.
- **166 Lawrence Road, Athlone (Ward 48):** rezoning from single residential to General Residential GR2, with departures.
- **4 Boulder's Place, Simon's Town (Ward 61):** consent for a hotel, with Council's approval and departures.
- **23 Belvedere Road, Claremont (Ward 58):** rezoning from single residential to Local Business, with consent for a place of instruction and departures.
- **64 Newlands Road, Claremont (Ward 59):** a title-deed restriction removed, rezoning to General Residential GR1, and subdivision.
- **67 and 69 Main Road, Diep River (Ward 73):** two properties consolidated, with the business-zoned parts rezoned from General Business GB1 to GB2.
- **23 Constantia Main Road (Ward 62):** rezoning from Limited Use to Utility zoning and a site development plan, with an added condition about a west-facing window.
- Title-deed restrictions or old approval conditions were also removed at **79 Belvedere Road, Claremont**, **33 Colenso Road, Bishopscourt**, **1 Collette Close, Constantia** and **13 Jeffcoat Avenue, Bergvliet**.
