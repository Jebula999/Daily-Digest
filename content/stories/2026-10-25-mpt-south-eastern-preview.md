The City's **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** for the south-eastern district meets online on **Tuesday 27 October at 08:30**. The tribunal decides land-use applications, such as rezonings, consent uses and departures from building rules, that are too large or too contested for officials to approve on their own.

## What's on the agenda

**Rezonings**

- **Strand (Ward 83):** a rezoning with departures at 105 Sarel Cilliers Street (Remainder Erf 3641 The Strand).
- **Tafelsig, Mitchells Plain (Ward 82):** a rezoning, consent use and permanent departures at 41 Paulsberg Street (Erf 28955).
- **Rustdal (Ward 14):** a rezoning, relaxation of title-deed restrictions and permanent departures at 10 Arum Road (Erf 96).

**Penalties for unauthorised building or land use**

Administrative penalties are amounts owners must pay before work or uses that went ahead without approval can be legalised. Thirteen are on the agenda:

| Area | Property |
|---|---|
| Gordon's Bay | 5 Black Forest Close, Fairview Golf Estate; 9 Paradise Close |
| Strand | 11 Jurgens Street; 20 Joseph Way (amended application) |
| Somerset West | 18 Arlington Crescent, Bakkershoogte; 34 Dirkie Uys Road, Audas Estate |
| Mitchells Plain | 39 Estelle Crescent, Beacon Valley; 4 Cycad Crescent, Eastridge |
| Rosedale, Klein Vlei | 32 Abraham Riddle Street |
| Blue Downs | 109 Government Road, Fairdale |
| Eersterivier (Ward 75) | 55 Barbara Road |
| Rustdal (Ward 14) | 11 Amandel Avenue |
| Khayelitsha | Portions of land bordered by the N2, Jeff Masemola Road and Icola Street, at Barnet Bolokwana Corner (the BM Sections 1–2 informal settlement) |

The Khayelitsha item is unusual: it concerns parts of three erven and a farm portion on which an informal settlement stands, rather than a single house. The agenda title does not say what the contravention is.

## How to follow it

The agenda and, after the meeting, the resolutions are published on the City's [Council Hub](https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/councilhubonline/councilandCommitteemeetings) under *Municipal Planning Tribunal*. Applicants and people who formally objected during the comment period are normally notified and may address the tribunal.
