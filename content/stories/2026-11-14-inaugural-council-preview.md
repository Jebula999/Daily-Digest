Cape Town's newly elected council holds its inaugural meeting on **Thursday 19 November at 10:00** in the Council Chamber at the Civic Centre. It is the first sitting since the 4 November local government elections, in which the DA won an outright majority in the city, Daily Maverick reported. The agenda, published on the City's Council Hub, sets out how the new term starts.

**Who is in the room.** A report confirming the type of municipality notes that the City has 235 councillors: 118 elected in wards and 117 from party lists. It also confirms that Cape Town keeps an executive mayoral system combined with subcouncils and ward participation.

**The elections.** The City Manager opens the meeting and chairs it until councillors elect a **speaker**, using the procedure in Schedule 3 of the Municipal Structures Act. The speaker then takes the chair for the election of the **executive mayor** and the **executive deputy mayor**, each followed by an address and responses from the parties. Councillors also elect a **chief whip**, and parties announce their own whips. Councillors make a solemn affirmation, and the code of conduct and declarations of interest are on the agenda.

**Full-time councillors and pay.** Officials recommend that the mayor, deputy mayor, speaker, mayoral committee members, chief whip, subcouncil chairs and chairs of portfolio committees be designated full-time councillors. Council is asked to note the national upper limits on councillors' salaries and allowances, which the previous council adopted in March 2026.

**Setting up the term.** Other reports deal with the council's rules of order, its system of delegating powers to officials and committees, the establishment of committees and the start of the process to set up subcouncils. Council will also consider a process plan for drafting the Integrated Development Plan for the new term of office (the City's main planning document), the City's language policy, a calendar of council and committee meetings up to December 2027, and the administration's handover report to the new council.

**How to follow.** Agendas and reports are on the [Council Hub](https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/councilhubonline/councilandCommitteemeetings), filed under the 19 November meeting.
