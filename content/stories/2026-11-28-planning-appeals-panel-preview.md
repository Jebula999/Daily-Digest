The City's **Planning Appeals Advisory Panel** meets on **Wednesday 2 December at 09:00** in the Ray Alexander Simon Room at the Civic Centre. The panel hears appeals against decisions of the Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT) and of City officials on land-use applications. It has no decision-making power of its own: it makes recommendations to the Executive Mayor, who is the appeal authority. The panel is also asked to adopt formal terms of reference setting out that role.

## The data centre appeal

The largest item concerns **90 Pallotti Road, King David Country Club** (Erf 178931-RE and neighbouring erven), part of the King Air Industria development. In July the MPT approved an amendment to the 2019 conditions of approval so that already-approved floor area can be reallocated to a **second data centre**, along with the rezoning of a small erf from Mixed Use 1 to General Industry 1, a subdivision and a consolidation into one land unit for the facility.

One objector's appeal was lodged, on behalf of the Legal Resources Centre, and four further parties were granted intervener status in October. According to the agenda, the appellants question the transparency of the process and the MPT's reasoning, and raise the data centre's water and electricity use. The applicant says in its response that the existing water allocation for the site is enough, because the facility will use air cooling, and that no extra municipal water allocation is expected; the appellants argue that air-cooled facilities can still need water and that data centres use water indirectly through electricity generation. The City's land-use district office says its position has not changed and it supports the MPT's approval. Some of the appellants will address the panel in person.
