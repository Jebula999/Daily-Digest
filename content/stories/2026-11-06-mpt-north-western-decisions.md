The **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** is the City panel that decides rezonings and departures from building rules, and sets penalties for building without approval. Its north-western branch met online on Tuesday 3 November ([what was on the agenda](/stories/2026-10-28-mpt-north-western-preview/)). Almost all decisions were unanimous; two penalties were decided by a vote.

## Refused and referred back

- **18 Campbell Road, Observatory (Ward 57):** a permanent departure from building rules and the City approval sought for the property were both refused.
- **3 Woodside Close, Tamboerskloof (Ward 77):** City approval under the Development Management Scheme was granted, but the departure that was also sought was refused.
- **7 Hofmeyr Street, Gardens (Ward 77):** referred back. The panel wants the application advertised more widely and its concerns addressed on heritage impact, height departures, wall-plate height and how the building meets the neighbouring property. The same panel will hear it again at a special meeting, where the applicant and objector can speak again.

## Approved

- **9 Namar Road, Sanddrift (Ward 55):** rezoned from single residential to Local Business 2, with consent for business premises, subject to conditions.
- **2A Kei Street, Milnerton (Ward 55):** consent for a guesthouse, with departures.
- **12 Barbara Road, Camps Bay (Ward 54):** restrictive title-deed conditions removed and amended.
- **29 Oldfield Road, Sea Point East (Ward 54):** one parking bay instead of six, with cars allowed to reverse across the pavement, to allow a change of land use in the heritage overlay zone.
- **74 Upper Park Road and 81 Chamberlain Street, Woodstock (Ward 57):** City approval and departures.
- **12 Exeter Way, Pinelands (Ward 53):** title-deed conditions removed.
- **17 Sunrise Road, Maitland (Ward 53):** conditions of an earlier approval amended.
- **Tijgerhof, Table View and Blouberg Sands:** title-deed conditions removed or departures approved at 5 Bremer Street, 13 Sprigg Road and 25 Keel Close. At Keel Close, the panel refused one departure, a 1.24m setback for a staircase on the northern boundary.

## Penalties

When owners build without permission and then apply to legalise it, the tribunal sets an "administrative penalty".

| Area | Amount | Vote |
|---|---|---|
| 5 & 7 Houghton Road and 139 & 141 Victoria Road, Camps Bay | R200,000 | unanimous |
| 108 Buitenkant Street, Gardens | R100,000 | 3 to 2 |
| 12 Arcadia Road, Fresnaye | R65,000 | unanimous |
| 13 Invermark Crescent, Gardens | R20,000 | unanimous |
| 25 Third Beach Road, Clifton | R8,000 | 4 to 1 |
| Saxon Crescent, West Beach | R7,400 | unanimous |
| 77 Mountain Road, Woodstock | R6,500 | unanimous |
| 77 Regent Road, Parklands | R3,200 | unanimous |
| 9 Newbury Crescent, Milnerton Ridge | R1,629 | unanimous |
| 55 Chiappini Street, Bo-Kaap | R1,000 | unanimous |
| 4 Rosedene Road, Sea Point | R0 | unanimous |

In Fresnaye, the panel noted that building work carried on after stop-work notices were served. In Buitenkant Street, it counted the fact that construction has stopped, and that the owner had no previous contraventions on record, in reducing the amount. The West Beach item is listed as 130 Saxon Crescent but the resolution gives 30 Saxon Crescent.

## What happens next

Tribunal decisions can be appealed, and the City's Planning Appeals Advisory Panel advises on those appeals. The full resolutions, with conditions and reasons, are on the City's [Council Hub](https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/councilhubonline/councilandCommitteemeetings) under *Municipal Planning Tribunal*.
