The **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** is the City panel that decides rezonings and departures from building rules, and sets penalties for building without approval. Its north-eastern branch, covering areas such as Bellville, Durbanville, Goodwood, Kuils River and the northern farmlands, met online on Tuesday 10 November ([what was on the agenda](/stories/2026-11-04-mpt-north-eastern-preview/)). Every decision recorded was unanimous except the Goodwood house shop, where no vote count is given.

## Refused

- **119 Hamilton Street, Goodwood Estate (Ward 27):** consent to run a house shop was refused.

## Approved

- **Farm 728, Joostenbergvlakte (Ward 102):** six farm portions consolidated and rezoned from transport and agricultural zoning to a subdivisional area overlay, with a master landscaping plan, subject to conditions. The overlay is the zoning step that allows a large site to be subdivided for development later.
- **41 Owl Street, Joostenbergvlakte Smallholdings (Ward 102):** a freestanding cellphone mast, with departures allowing it on the western and Owl Street boundaries (0m setback instead of 5m and 10m).
- **23 Magnolia Crescent, Valmary Park (Ward 21):** title-deed and township conditions removed and the property subdivided.
- **3 Van der Stel Street, Kingston, Bellville (Ward 10):** a title-deed condition and a condition of an earlier approval removed.
- **12 Visserhof Road, Bothasig (Ward 5):** consent for a guesthouse, with departures.
- **57 Bellhome Street, Highbury, Kuils River (Ward 19):** subdivision into two portions.

## Penalties

When owners build or change a property's use without permission and then apply to legalise it, the tribunal sets an "administrative penalty". All were decided unanimously.

## What happens next

Tribunal decisions can be appealed, and the City's Planning Appeals Advisory Panel advises on those appeals. The full resolutions, with conditions and reasons, are on the City's [Council Hub](https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/councilhubonline/councilandCommitteemeetings) under *Municipal Planning Tribunal*.
