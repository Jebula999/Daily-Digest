The City's **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** for the south-western district meets online on **Tuesday 17 November at 08:30**. The tribunal rules on land-use applications, such as rezonings, subdivisions, consent uses and departures from building rules, that are too large or too contested for officials to decide alone. There are 48 items on the agenda.

## What's on the agenda

**Claremont, Rondebosch and Newlands (Wards 57, 58 and 59)**

- **4 Feldhausen Road, Claremont (Erf 55177):** rezoning and permanent departures.
- **23 Belvedere Road, Claremont (Erf 51323):** rezoning, a consent use and departures.
- **79 Belvedere Road, Claremont (Erf 51890):** deletion of title-deed restrictions, changes to conditions of an existing approval, Council's approval, a consent use and departures.
- **64 Newlands Road, Claremont (Remainder Erf 56314):** deletion of a title-deed restriction, rezoning, subdivision, departures and Council's approval.
- **2 Lyle Road, Rosebank (Erf 158504):** subdivision and departures.

**Cape Flats (Wards 44, 48 and 66)**

- **166 Lawrence Road, Athlone (Erf 38437):** rezoning and permanent departures.
- **Erf 6482, Grassy Park:** a deviation from the Cape Flats District Plan 2023, rezoning and subdivision, with approval to build out the subdivision in phases.
- **15 Vukuzenzele Street, Gugulethu (Erf 9967):** a temporary land-use departure.

**Southern suburbs and the far south (Wards 61, 62 and 73)**

- **67 and 69 Main Road, Diep River:** rezoning, consolidation, a consent use, Council's approval and departures.
- **23 Constantia Main Road (Remainder Erf 8992 Constantia):** rezoning and Council's approval.
- **33 Colenso Road, Bishopscourt (Erf 56603):** removal of a title-deed restriction and a departure.
- **1 Collette Close, Constantia (Erf 678):** deletion of title-deed restrictions and of conditions of an existing approval.
- **13 Jeffcoat Avenue, Bergvliet (Erf 255):** departures and deletion of title-deed restrictions and approval conditions.
- **4 Boulder's Place, Simon's Town (Erf 2298):** a consent use, Council's approval and departures.
