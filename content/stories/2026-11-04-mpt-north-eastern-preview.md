The City's **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** for the north-eastern district meets online on **Tuesday 10 November at 08:30**. The tribunal decides land-use applications, such as rezonings, subdivisions, consent uses and departures from building rules, that are too large or too contested for officials to approve on their own. There are 27 items on the agenda.

## What's on the agenda

**Joostenbergvlakte (Ward 102)**

- **Farm 728, Joostenbergvlakte:** consolidation, rezoning and subdivision of portions 29, 30, 32, 374, 375 and 377, with consent in terms of title-deed restrictions and approval of a master landscaping plan.
- **41 Owl Street, Joostenbergvlakte Smallholdings (Portion 111 of Farm 728, Paarl Farms):** a consent use and departures for a freestanding cellphone mast.

**Durbanville, Bellville and Kuils River**

- **Valmary Park (Ward 21):** removal of title-deed restrictions, deletion of township conditions and a subdivision at 23 Magnolia Crescent (Erf 623 Eversdale).
- **Kingston, Bellville (Ward 10):** removal of a title-deed restriction and of an existing condition of approval at 3 Van der Stel Street (Erf 5538).
- **Highbury, Kuils River (Ward 19):** subdivision at 57 Bellhome Street (Erf 9345).

**Bothasig and Goodwood**

- **Bothasig (Ward 5):** a consent use and departures at 12 Visserhof Road (Erf 7772 Milnerton).
- **Goodwood Estate (Ward 27):** a consent application at 119 Hamilton Street (Erf 13836).
