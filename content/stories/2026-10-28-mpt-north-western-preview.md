The City's **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** for the north-western district meets online on **Tuesday 3 November at 08:30**, the day before the local elections. The tribunal decides land-use applications, such as rezonings, consent uses and departures from building rules, that are too large or too contested for officials to approve on their own. There are 27 items on the agenda.

## What's on the agenda

**Observatory, Woodstock and the City Bowl**

- **Observatory (Ward 57):** three applications for departures and City approval: 18 Campbell Road (Erf 149979), 24 Liskeard Road (Erf 28012) and 2 Willow Road (Erf 178828).
- **Woodstock (Ward 57):** departures and City approval at 74 Upper Park Road (Erf 161463-RE, amended) and 81 Chamberlain Street (Erf 14184).
- **Tamboerskloof and Gardens (Ward 77):** departures at 3 Woodside Close (Erf 1317 Tamboerskloof) and 7 Hofmeyr Street (Remainder Erf 1308 Oranjezicht).

**Atlantic Seaboard (Ward 54)**

- **Camps Bay:** removal and amendment of title-deed and land-use restrictions, with departures, at 12 Barbara Road (Erf 2618, amended).
- **Sea Point East:** City approval and departures at 29 Oldfield Road (Erf 360, amended).

**Milnerton, Table View and Pinelands**

- **Tijgerhof (Ward 55):** removal of title restrictions and departures at 5 Bremer Street (Erf 18660).
- **Sanddrift (Ward 55):** a rezoning, consent use and departure at 9 Namar Road (Erf 105853).
- **Milnerton (Ward 55):** a consent use and departure at 2A Kei Street (Erf 1237).
- **Blouberg Sands (Ward 107):** departures at 25 Keel Close (Erf 25776).
- **Table View (Ward 113):** removal of title-deed and township conditions, with a departure, at 13 Sprigg Road (Erf 5467).
- **Pinelands (Ward 53):** removal of title-deed and land-use restrictions, with departures, at 12 Exeter Way (Erf 1278).
- **Maitland (Ward 53):** a change to the conditions of an earlier approval at 17 Sunrise Road (Erf 110934).

## How to follow it

The agenda and, after the meeting, the resolutions are published on the City's [Council Hub](https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/councilhubonline/councilandCommitteemeetings) under *Municipal Planning Tribunal*. Applicants and people who formally objected during the comment period are normally notified and may address the tribunal.
