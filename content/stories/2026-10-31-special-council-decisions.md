The City's full council met online on **Thursday 29 October** in a sitting that lasted just over an hour ([what was on the agenda](/stories/2026-10-27-special-council-29-october-preview/)). The minutes list the following decisions.

## What was decided

- **Adjustments budget:** adopted. The ANC, Africa Restoration Alliance, Al Jama-ah, Cape Independence Party, National Coloured Congress, Democratic Independence, FF Plus, GOOD and the Patriotic Alliance recorded votes against.
- **Water mains contract:** a five-year term tender for new water mains, fire hydrants and valves, estimated at R182.5 million before contingencies and VAT, was approved. Al Jama-ah, the Africa Restoration Alliance and GOOD voted against.
- **Flood insurance:** council noted the work done with National Treasury and the World Bank, approved parametric insurance in principle and authorised officials to test the market and procure a product. Parametric insurance pays an agreed amount once a measured trigger is reached. Any final proposal and its cost must still come back for approval.
- **Fynbos Dam interest:** council authorised interest of up to R105,150.78 owed to a contractor after an adjudicator's decision on a principal of about R1.5 million. The Patriotic Alliance voted against and GOOD abstained. One further claim is still being assessed.
- **Audit committees:** members of the audit committees for Cape Town Stadium and the Cape Town International Convention Centre were changed.

## Event support and grants

| Item | Amount | Recorded positions |
|---|---|---|
| SA20 cricket league finals, 2026/27 and 2028/29 | up to R2.5 million in each year | CCC and FF Plus supported "with concern"; GOOD and ARA abstained |
| Wynberg Family Festival, three years from 26 March 2027 | up to R850,000, R857,500 and R906,375 | GOOD against |
| Young Urbanists NPC (Open Streets), 2026/27 | R600,000 | CCC and GOOD abstained |

One GOOD councillor was removed from the online meeting under the rules of order during debate on the SA20 item. The minutes do not give the reason.

## What happens next

Council also noted the quarterly financial report to September, withdrawals from municipal bank accounts and procurement deviations. The ordinary portfolio committees and mayoral committee set for early November are cancelled; the Municipal Public Accounts Committee is next on the calendar, on 10 November.
