The City's **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** for the south-eastern district meets online on **Tuesday 24 November at 08:30**. The tribunal rules on land-use applications, such as rezonings, subdivisions, consent uses and departures from building rules, that are too large or too contested for officials to decide alone. After the procedural items, the agenda lists 21 matters: nine land-use applications and twelve administrative penalties.

## What's on the agenda

**Helderberg: Somerset West, Gordon's Bay and Faure**

- **9 Swalle Street, Golden Acre, Somerset West (Erven 2390 and 2391):** consolidation, rezoning, permanent departures and a site development plan.
- **93 Andries Pretorius Street, Audas Estate, Somerset West (Erf 1081):** rezoning and a permanent departure.
- **143 Main Road, Somerset West (Remainder Erf 853 and Erf 854):** subdivision and consolidation (Ward 84).
- **71 Stone Street, Lourensia Park, Somerset West (Erf 19542):** amendment of a condition of approval, a consent use and permanent departures.
- **Corner of Roos and Avondrus Streets, Gordon's Bay (Erven 1409 and 558):** rezoning, consolidation, departures and changes to an approved site development plan.
- **Portions 228 and 237 of Farm Firland No. 959, near Gordon's Bay (Ward 100):** consolidation, rezoning, subdivision, a departure and a site development plan. The application also asks to deviate from the Municipal Spatial Development Framework, the Helderberg District Plan and the Gordon's Bay local spatial development framework, the City's long-term plans for where and how the area should grow.
- **Farm 654, Portions 8 and 10, Faure (Ward 90):** consolidation, rezoning and subdivision.

**Elsewhere**

- **1 Tambotie Road, Wildwood (Portion 67 of Cape Farm 787, Ward 75):** rezoning, subdivision, a site development plan, amended conditions and permanent departures.
- **22 Gourley Road, Penhill (Erf 222, Ward 14):** subdivision.

## How to follow it

Tribunal meetings are held on Microsoft Teams. The agenda and, after the meeting, the decisions are published on the City's [Council Hub](https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/councilhubonline/councilandCommitteemeetings). People who commented on an application during public participation are usually told how to take part. The meeting appears as "rescheduled" on the City's calendar, so check the Council Hub for the final date and time.
