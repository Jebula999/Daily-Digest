The City's full council meets in a **special sitting on Thursday 29 October at 10:00**, online. The ordinary council meeting set for the same day was cancelled. Most items come to council as recommendations from the Executive Mayor and mayoral committee, agreed by "round robin" (signed off in writing rather than at a meeting).

## What's on the agenda

- **Adjustments budget (item 3).** The law allows a municipality to amend its budget during the year. This one is small: it adds **R6 million** from the national Municipal Disaster Response Grant to repair community facilities damaged in the April and May 2026 floods, with the same amount added to spending, and records transfers between capital projects made up to 5 October. The City's total spending for 2026/27 comes to about R74.6bn, and the capital budget stays at about R13.4bn.
- **Flood insurance that pays out automatically (item 7).** Council is asked to approve "in principle" the use of parametric insurance, which pays an agreed amount as soon as a measured trigger is reached, such as a set rainfall level, instead of after a long assessment of losses. The product was developed with National Treasury and the World Bank. The report says the July 2024 floods cost the City and its partners about R255 million directly, and informal settlements an estimated R462 million. Council would only authorise the administration to test the market; any actual policy and its cost would come back for approval.
- **Water mains contract (item 5).** A five-year term tender for laying new water mains, fire hydrants and valves, estimated at **R182.5 million** before contingencies and VAT. Council must approve contracts that run beyond the three-year budget cycle.
- **Fynbos Dam interest (item 6).** After an adjudicator ruled in August that the City owes a contractor R1.5 million for work on the Fynbos Dam safety upgrade, council is asked to approve interest on that amount of up to about R105,000. One further claim is still being assessed.
- **Event support (items 8 and 9).** Up to **R2.5 million** in cash sponsorship each for the SA20 cricket league finals in the 2026/27 and 2028/29 seasons, starting with the final on 20 February 2027, plus possible waivers of parking and poster fees. Separately, a three-year agreement for the **Wynberg Family Festival**, from 26 March 2027, worth up to R850,000 in the first year and rising each year after that.
- **Open Streets funding (item 10).** A **R600,000** grant to Young Urbanists NPC for six months of car-free Sundays on **Bree Street** in the city centre and **Lerotholo Avenue in Langa**, from 25 October 2026 to 28 March 2027. The report says the application missed the normal grants process because of an administrative oversight, so money has to be moved from another budget line.

Council also receives, for information, the quarterly financial report to September, withdrawals from municipal bank accounts and a report on procurement deviations, and is asked to change members of the audit committees of the City's municipal entities.
