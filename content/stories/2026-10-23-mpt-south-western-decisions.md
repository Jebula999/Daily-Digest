The **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** is the City panel of officials and outside experts that decides rezonings, subdivisions and departures from building rules, and sets penalties for building without approval. Its south-western branch, covering the southern suburbs, the far south and much of the Cape Flats, met online on Tuesday 20 October ([what was on the agenda](/stories/2026-10-15-mpt-south-western-preview/)). All but one of its decisions were unanimous.

## Refused

- **Glendirk Farm, Klaassens Road, Constantia (Ward 62):** the tribunal refused to subdivide Remainder Erf 10373, and refused to rezone part of it for a single residential plot with the rest zoned rural.
- **4A Breton Close, Hout Bay (Ward 74):** a subdivision, a consent use and a departure were all refused.

## Approved

- **6 Duntaw Close and 8 Bel Ombre Road, Constantia (Ward 62):** the two properties (Erf 803 and Remainder Erf 123) were rezoned to a subdivisional area, the zoning that allows land to be laid out into new plots. Remainder Erf 123 was partly zoned open space.
- **109 Southern Cross Drive, Constantia (Ward 62):** title-deed restrictions were suspended and amended, and a condition of an earlier approval changed, clearing the way for a subdivision.
- **7 Evergreen Lane, Constantia (Ward 62):** a subdivision was approved, on condition that a site development plan showing how new building avoids the trees to be kept is approved before building plans.
- **57 Rhodes Avenue, Newlands (Ward 62):** title-deed restrictions were deleted and a subdivision approved, but a requested departure from building rules was refused.
- **56 Dean Street, Rondebosch (Ward 59):** rezoned from single residential to General Residential 2, which allows a block of flats, with a further council approval under the Development Management Scheme.
- **173 Princess Vlei Road, Southfield (Ward 72):** a subdivision and departures were approved.
- **17 Fish Road, Zeekoevlei:** permanent departures were approved.

## Penalties

When owners build or use property without permission and then apply to legalise it, the tribunal sets an "administrative penalty" before the application can go ahead.

| Area | Amount | For |
|---|---|---|
| Willow Road, Constantia | R20,000 | a 225m² parking area used by a guest house on the neighbouring property |
| Turkey Berry Lane, Hout Bay | R15,000 | unauthorised building work |

- **101 Ottery Road, Philippi (Ward 43):** the subdivisions of two farm portions (Portions 0 and 2 of Cape Farm 670) were approved, along with the related consolidation with Portion 3 of Cape Farm 729.
- **96 Mpinga Street, Gugulethu (Ward 39):** a consent use and a departure for a freestanding telecommunications base station were refused.

## What happens next

Tribunal decisions can be appealed, and the City's Planning Appeals Advisory Panel advises on those appeals. The full resolutions, with the conditions of approval and reasons, are on the City's [Council Hub](https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/councilhubonline/councilandCommitteemeetings) under *Municipal Planning Tribunal*.
