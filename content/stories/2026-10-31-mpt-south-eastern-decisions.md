The **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** is the City panel that decides rezonings and departures from building rules, and sets penalties for building without approval. Its south-eastern branch met online on Tuesday 27 October ([what was on the agenda](/stories/2026-10-25-mpt-south-eastern-preview/)). Every decision was unanimous except the Strand rezoning.

## Rezonings

- **105 Sarel Cilliers Street, Strand (Ward 83):** approved by 3 votes to 1, from single residential to General Residential 2, a zoning for higher-density housing, with a departure allowing two driveway crossings instead of one.
- **10 Arum Road, Rustdal (Ward 14):** approved, from single residential to General Residential 2, with title-deed conditions relaxed and departures, subject to conditions.
- **41 Paulsberg Street, Tafelsig (Ward 82):** rezoning to Local Business refused. The application also sought consent to sell liquor for consumption off the premises.

## Penalties

When owners build or use property without permission and then apply to legalise it, the tribunal sets an "administrative penalty".

## What happens next

Tribunal decisions can be appealed, and the City's Planning Appeals Advisory Panel advises on those appeals. The full resolutions, with conditions and reasons, are on the City's [Council Hub](https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/councilhubonline/councilandCommitteemeetings) under *Municipal Planning Tribunal*.
