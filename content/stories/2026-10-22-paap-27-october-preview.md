The City's **Planning Appeals Advisory Panel** meets on **Tuesday 27 October at 09:00** in the Ray Alexander Simon Room at the Civic Centre. When someone appeals a planning decision, whether made by an official or by a Municipal Planning Tribunal, the panel hears the case and advises the Executive Mayor, who is the appeal authority and makes the final call. This meeting has 20 appeals (items 29 to 48).

## The cases to watch

- **Green Point, Scholtz Road:** two appeals against tribunal decisions to *refuse* departures from building rules, at 10 Scholtz Road (erf 942) and 8 Scholtz Road (erf 2250). Each comes with a bundle of more than 500 pages, the largest on the agenda.
- **Sea Point, 38 Blackheath Road:** an appeal against a tribunal decision to *approve* City approval and permanent departures.
- **Milnerton, 13 Riverside Mews and 4 Helwan Road:** an appeal against an approved change to a condition about the Diep River floodplain. The original 2020 approval barred any building below the 1-in-100-year flood line; the amended condition requires floor levels above that line but only bars structures below the 1-in-50-year line, allowing a larger area to be built on.
- **Fish Hoek, 44 Chebec Crescent, Sun Valley:** an appeal against approved departures that allow a second and third dwelling on a 671 square-metre single-residential plot, with reduced setbacks and a taller building and boundary wall.
- **Hout Bay, 30 Nooitgedacht Drive:** a neighbour appeals against an approved departure, arguing that a raised pool and patio next to the shared boundary would overlook their property.
- **Bergvliet, 11 William Frederick Road:** an appeal against a split decision on a street boundary wall. The official allowed a slightly higher wall but refused a solid one, saying a wall with no see-through sections would harm the streetscape and street safety.

## Other appeals

Appeals against approvals in **Heideveld** (17A Jonkershoek Weg), **Claremont** (3 Midwood Avenue), **Kirstenhof** (39 Summer Place, a tribunal approval), **Welgemoed** (7 Van de Graaf Street, a tribunal approval), **Pinelands** (16 Francis Road), **Gordon's Bay** (17 Kloof Road), **Gugulethu** (88 and 89 Solomon Mahlangu Crescent, a consolidation and rezoning), **Constantia** (22 Bergendal Road), **Simon's Town** (24 Rocklands Road), **Steenberg** (18 Lomond Crescent) and **Newlands** (1 Lady Anne Avenue). Two appeals are against refusals: **Belhar** (95 Banjo Walk) and a Joostenbergvlakte smallholding at 25 Wagtail Street, Kraaifontein.

## How to follow

The agenda and full reports for each item are on the City's Council Hub under the meeting date. Appellants and applicants are normally notified of the hearing directly; anyone else interested in a case can contact the case officer named in that item's report.
