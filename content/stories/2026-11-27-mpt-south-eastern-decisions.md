The **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** is the City panel that decides rezonings, subdivisions and departures from building rules, and sets penalties for building without approval. Its south-eastern branch, which covers the Helderberg and the eastern Cape Flats, met online on Tuesday 24 November ([what was on the agenda](/stories/2026-11-18-mpt-south-eastern-preview/)). Every decision was unanimous except one penalty in Klein Vlei, decided by three votes to one. In each case the tribunal said it followed the reasons in the planner's report.

## Refused

- **Portions 228 and 237 of Farm Firland No. 959, corner of Sir Lowry's Pass Road, Hydro Road and Windsor Road (Ward 100):** the tribunal refused to consolidate the two farm portions into a 2.3-hectare site and refused to rezone it from agriculture to General Business. The application had also asked to deviate from the City's spatial plans for the Helderberg and Gordon's Bay, which set out where and how the area should grow.

## Approved

- **9 Swalle Street, Golden Acre, Somerset West:** two properties consolidated and rezoned from single residential to General Residential GR2, allowing **30 senior living units**, with departures and a site development plan.
- **93 Andries Pretorius Street, Audas Estate, Somerset West:** rezoning from General Residential GR2 to Local Business, with parking cut from 12 bays to 9.
- **143 Main Road, Somerset West (Ward 84):** part of one property subdivided off and joined to its neighbour.
- **71 Stone Street, Lourensia Park, Somerset West:** a 2010 condition on the Lourensia Park development amended to allow a house shop, with a consent use and departures.
- **Corner of Roos and Avondrus Streets, Gordon's Bay:** a residential property rezoned to General Business and consolidated with its neighbour, with changes to an approved site development plan.
- **Farm 654, Portions 8 and 10, Faure (Ward 90):** two farm portions consolidated and rezoned from agriculture to a subdivisional area, a zoning used before land is divided up for development.
- **1 Tambotie Road, Wildwood (Ward 75):** a substation site subdivided off the property and rezoned for utility use.
- **22 Gourley Road, Penhill (Ward 14):** a subdivision.
