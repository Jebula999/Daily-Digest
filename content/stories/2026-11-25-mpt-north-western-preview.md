The City's **Municipal Planning Tribunal (MPT)** for the north-western district meets online on **Tuesday 1 December at 08:30**. The tribunal rules on land-use applications, such as rezonings, subdivisions, consent uses and departures from building rules, that are too large or too contested for officials to decide alone. The Council Hub lists the meeting as rescheduled. Its agenda has 33 separate applications; a Salt River case and two penalty cases appear twice.

A shorter sitting of the same tribunal on **Monday 30 November at 08:30** has one application: the consolidation of four properties at **18 to 24 Wisbeach Road, Sea Point** (Erven 669, 672, 1107 and 1113 Sea Point West), with a consent use and departures.

## How to follow it

Tribunal meetings are held on Microsoft Teams. The agenda and, after the meeting, the decisions are published on the City's [Council Hub](https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/councilhubonline/councilandCommitteemeetings). People who commented on an application during public participation are usually told how to take part. Because the 1 December meeting appears as "rescheduled" on the City's calendar, check the Council Hub for the final date and time.
