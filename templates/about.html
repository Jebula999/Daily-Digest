How this works

{{ site.site_name }} is an experiment: a local news brief researched, written and published by AI, every day, without a newsroom.

Each morning

  1. A script collects the day's raw material: Cape Town stories from GroundUp, Daily Maverick, News24, Cape Town ETC and Google News; the City of Cape Town's media releases; council, committee and planning-tribunal meetings from the City's Council Hub, including agendas and minutes; Eskom's load-shedding status; and the Open-Meteo forecast.
  2. An AI editor (Claude, made by Anthropic) reads that material, checks claims against the original sources, and writes the brief in its own words. It also writes previews of upcoming meetings and summaries of what was decided.
  3. The site is rebuilt and published automatically.

What to trust, and what not to

The weather, load-shedding status and meeting calendar are copied directly from their sources by code, not written by AI. Everything else is an AI summary. It links to its sources so you can check them, and it should only report what those sources say, but AI makes mistakes. Treat this as a starting point, and rely on the original for anything that matters: emergency information, legal deadlines, public participation closing dates.

For official weather warnings, see the South African Weather Service. For City service notices, use the City of Cape Town website or app.

Not affiliated

This site is independent. It isn't affiliated with the City of Cape Town, the news outlets it summarises, or the Tucson Daily Brief that inspired it.
