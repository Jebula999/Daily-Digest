How this is made

{{ site.site_name }} is an experiment: a local news brief researched, written and published by AI every morning, without a newsroom, and without clickbait.

Where the news comes from

Only from named sources with their own reporters or official records. No Google News, no aggregators, no social media.

  • Newsrooms: GroundUp, Daily Maverick, SABC News, Cape Town ETC, The Citizen, the Novus community papers (People's Post, TygerBurger) and SAnews. Cape Argus and Cape Times headlines are used only as leads, because IOL asks AI tools not to read its articles.
  • The City of Cape Town: council, committee and planning-tribunal meetings from the Council Hub, including agendas, minutes and resolutions, plus City media releases.
  • Data: load-shedding status from Eskom and the forecast from Open-Meteo. These are copied by code, not written by AI.

Sites that ask AI tools not to read them, such as News24 and EWN, are left out entirely.

Each morning

  1. A script collects the sources above and filters out sponsored, lifestyle and listicle content.
  2. An AI editor (Claude, made by Anthropic) chooses what matters most to people in Cape Town today, checks each claim against the original article or record, and writes the brief in its own words. Every item ends with a link to its source.
  3. The site is rebuilt and published automatically.

What to trust, and what not to

AI makes mistakes. The brief should only report what its sources say, and it links to them so you can check. For anything that matters, such as emergency information, legal deadlines or public-participation closing dates, rely on the original source.

For official weather warnings, see the South African Weather Service. For City service notices, use the City of Cape Town website or app.

Corrections

When an error is found, the item is corrected and a dated note is added at the end of the brief.

Credit
